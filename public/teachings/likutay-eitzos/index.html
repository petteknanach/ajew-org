About Likutey Eitzos

Likutey Eitzos (Collected Advice) is a compilation of practical guidance from Rabbi Nachman of Breslov, organized alphabetically by topic. It contains essential advice for spiritual growth, daily life, and connecting with God.

This bilingual edition presents the original Hebrew alongside English translation.
