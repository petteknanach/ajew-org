Hisbodidus is a super way of receiving Divine help despite the restriction of “free choice.” When he (Rabbi Nachman) made known the matter of hisbodidus and conversing between oneself and his Creator, our teacher, the rav, the tzadik Rabbi Nussun zatza”l spoke with him, and asked him: Behold a person has free choice (- t.n. so how can simply relating matters to Hashem effectuate the changes which should be incumbent on the person himself to achieve) ? And he did not answer him explicitly, just offhandedly, as if to say, even still. That is to say, that is impossible to explain this matter to you completely, (and) even still it is necessary to practice this.
