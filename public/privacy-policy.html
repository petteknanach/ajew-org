Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Ajew mobile application ("App," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our App. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

The Ajew App is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information to provide and improve the App experience:

Data Type Purpose Collection Method
App Usage Data To improve app performance and fix bugs Anonymous crash reports and analytics
Device Information To ensure compatibility and optimize performance Device type, OS version, screen size
Content Preferences To personalize your reading experience Your selected books, reading progress

3. What We Do NOT Collect

We want to be transparent about what we do NOT collect:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or address
  • No Location Data: We do not track your geographical location
  • No Contacts: We do not access your contact list
  • No Photos/Media: We do not access your photos, camera, or media files
  • No Payment Information: The App is free with no in-app purchases

4. Data Storage

All your reading preferences and progress are stored locally on your device. We do not store this information on our servers. The only data transmitted to us is anonymous usage statistics for app improvement.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • App Store Analytics: Basic download and usage statistics from Apple App Store/Google Play Store
  • Crash Reporting: Anonymous crash reports to fix app issues

6. Children's Privacy

The Ajew App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The content is educational and spiritual in nature.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect the limited information we collect. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any information we have about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of analytics collection (through device settings)
  • Uninstall the App at any time, which removes all locally stored data

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the App after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
