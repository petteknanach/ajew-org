64 - And Hashem Said to Moses, Come to Pharaoh (Bo El Paroh - Enter the Realm of Impurity)

And Hashem said to Moses, Come to Pharaoh, for I have hardened his heart and the heart of his servants, that I may place these My signs in his midst, and that you may tell in the ears of your son and your son's son what I have done in Egypt, and My signs which I have placed in them, and you shall know that I am Hashem, etc. Behold, I am bringing tomorrow locusts in your border [Exodus 10:1-4] [See this Torah in another version from the manuscript of our Rabbi z"l himself] [Note: This teaching explores divine intention in creation and overcoming heresy through faith.] 1. For Hashem blessed, because of His mercy, created the world, for He wanted to reveal His mercy, and if there was no creation of the world, upon whom would He show His mercy? And therefore, He created all the creation from the beginning of emanation until the end of the central point of the physical world, to show His mercy, and when Hashem blessed wanted to create the world, there was no place to create it because all was infinite, therefore He contracted His light to the sides, and through this contraction (tzimtzum) was made the empty space (chalal panui - void for creation), and within this empty space were formed all the days and attributes, which are the creation of the world (as written in Etz Chaim at its beginning), and this empty space was necessary for the creation of the world, for without the empty space, there was no place for the creation of the world as above, and this contraction of the empty space is impossible to understand and attain, except in the future to come, for one must say in it two opposites, being and nothingness, for the empty space is through the contraction that, so to speak, He contracted His divinity from there, and there is no divinity there, so to speak, for if not, it is not empty, and all is infinite, and there is no place for the creation of the world at all, but in true truth, certainly even so there is divinity there, for certainly there is no thing without His vitality, and therefore it is impossible to attain the aspect of the empty space at all, until the future to come. 2. And know, that there are two kinds of epicureanism (apikorsut - heresy), there is epicureanism that comes from external wisdoms, and about it is said (Avot chapter 2): And know what to answer to an epicure, for this epicureanism has an answer, for this epicureanism comes from external wisdoms that come from surpluses (motarot - excesses) from the aspect of the breaking of the vessels, for because of the abundance of light, the vessels broke, and from there were formed the klipot (shells - husks of impurity) as known, and external wisdoms come from there, that is, from the breaking of the vessels, from surpluses, refuse of holiness, like in a person, there are several kinds of surpluses and refuse, such as nails and hair and sweat, and other refuse and surpluses, so every external wisdom comes from surpluses and known refuse of holiness, and similarly sorcery comes from surpluses and known refuse, and therefore one who falls to this epicureanism, even though certainly he needs to flee and escape from there, but even so, one who falls there, it is possible for him to find salvation to go out from there, for he can find there Hashem blessed, if he seeks Him and searches for Him there, for since they come from the breaking of the vessels, there are there many sparks of holiness and many letters that broke and fell there as known, and therefore he can find there divinity and intellect to settle the questions of this epicureanism that comes from external wisdoms that come from surpluses from the breaking of the vessels, for there is there vitality of divinity, that is, intellect and letters that broke and fell there, and therefore this epicureanism has an answer, and about it is said and know what to answer to an epicure, but there is another kind of epicureanism, and they are the wisdoms that are not wisdoms, but because they are deep and they do not attain them, and because of this they seem like wisdoms, like for example when one says a false reasoning in Gemara, Rashi’s commentary, Tosafot, and because the scholar cannot settle the question that comes through this reasoning, through this it seems that he said a reasoning and great wisdom, even though in truth it is not reasoning at all, so there are many confusions and questions among the investigators that in truth are not wisdom at all, and the questions are null from the outset, but because there is no human intellect to settle them, through this they seem like wisdoms and questions, and in truth it is impossible to settle these questions, for these questions of this epicureanism come from the empty space, that there within the empty space there is no divinity, so to speak, and therefore these questions that come from there, from the aspect of the empty space, it is impossible in any way to find an answer for them, that is, to find there Hashem blessed, for if he found there also Hashem blessed, then it was not empty, and all was infinite as above, and therefore about this epicureanism, it is said "All who come there shall not return" [Proverbs 2:19], for there is no answer for this epicureanism since it comes from the empty space, that from there He contracted His divinity, so to speak, only Israel through faith pass over all the wisdoms, and even over this epicureanism that comes from the empty space, no investigation and wisdom, only with complete faith, for Hashem blessed fills all worlds and surrounds all worlds (Zohar Pinchas 225a), it is found that He is, so to speak, within all the worlds, and around all the worlds, and there needs to be a difference, so to speak, between the filling and the surrounding, for if not, then all is one, but through the aspect of the empty space, that from there He contracted His divinity, so to speak, and within it created all the creation, it is found that the empty space surrounds all the world, and Hashem blessed who surrounds all worlds surrounds also the empty space, and therefore it is proper to say fills all worlds, that is, all the creation that was created within the empty space, and also surrounds all worlds, that is, He surrounds also the empty space, and in between separates the empty space, that, so to speak, He contracted His divinity from there, and behold, through faith that they believe that Hashem blessed fills all worlds and surrounds all worlds, and since He surrounds all worlds, then also the empty space itself was formed from His wisdom blessed, and certainly in true truth there is His divinity there, only it is impossible to attain this, and to find there Hashem blessed as above, therefore they pass over all the wisdoms and the questions and the epicureanism that comes from there from the empty space, for they know that certainly it is impossible to find an answer for them, for if he found an answer for them, that is, he found in them Hashem blessed, then it was not the empty space, and it was not possible for the creation to be formed, but in true truth certainly there is an answer for them, and certainly there is His divinity there, but through investigations they sink there, for it is impossible to find there Hashem blessed since it is the aspect of the empty space, only they need to believe that Hashem blessed surrounds also it, and certainly in truth there is His divinity there, and therefore Israel are called Hebrews (Ivrim, from root (avar - to pass over)) for they pass with their faith over all the wisdoms, and even over the wisdoms that are not wisdoms, that is, the second epicureanism that comes from the empty space as above, and therefore Hashem blessed is called "G-d of the Hebrews" [Exodus 3:18], from the language of "Beyond the river" [Joshua 24:2] (Rashi Genesis 39 on verse "Hebrew man"), language of sides, that is, His divinity surrounds also the empty space that comes through the contraction that He contracted the light to the sides, and therefore Israel are called Hebrews, that through their faith that they believe that Hashem blessed is G-d of the Hebrews as above, they pass over all the wisdoms and over what are not wisdoms, that is, the second epicureanism as above, and therefore certainly from this second epicureanism certainly one needs to be more and more careful, to flee and escape from there, not to look and gaze at their words at all, for G-d forbid, certainly he will sink there, for about it is said: "All who come there shall not return" etc. as above. 3. But know, if there is a great tzadik who is the aspect of Moses, he needs specifically to look into the words of this epicureanism, and
