Likutay Moharan Torah 321

Hebrew Content

תורה המתחלת בבית ומסימת בלמד, 'ובתלתא שבשין', הינו אוריתא תליתאה, הוא 'עבד נשמתין ורוחין חדתין'. וה

English Translation

LIKUTAY MOHARAN II, TORAH 35: THOSE WHO STUDY TORAH ARE WORTHY TO KNOW THE FUTURE Those who study Torah [Torah] are worthy to know the future. And this is what is written: “Before, I knew” (Psalms 119:67). That I know beforehand what will be, and from where? “From Your testimonies”, that is, from the Torah.
