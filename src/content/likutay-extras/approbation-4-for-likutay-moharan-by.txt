# Approbation 4 for Likutay Moharan

## From Rabbi Moshe Leib of Sassov

To all who seek God:

The book Likutay Moharan has come into my hands, and I have found it to be a treasure of holy wisdom. The teachings combine depth with simplicity, mystery with practical guidance.

I was particularly moved by the teachings about joy and simplicity. In our generation, when hearts are heavy and spirits are low, these teachings come as a refreshing spring in a dry land.

The author has merited to reveal new pathways in Torah, pathways that lead directly to God's presence. Each teaching is a ladder set on earth whose top reaches the heavens.

I give my full support to this holy work and bless all who study it with open hearts.

May these teachings spread light throughout the world and bring us closer to complete redemption.

With blessings for spiritual success,
Moshe Leib son of Malka
Sassov, 15 Tammuz 5568 (1808)
