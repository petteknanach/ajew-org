Outpouring of the Soul

Outpouring of the Soul - Breslov teachings on prayer and meditation
Pray for every single thing, G-d is “good for it.” Prayer is the hishtadlus ! It is necessary to accustom oneself to pray at all times for everything he is lacking; whether it be livelihood, or children, or when he has someone sick Heaven forbid, at home who needs healing etc., for everything his main aitza (advice, remedy) is just to pray to Hashem Yisburach, and to have faith in Hashem Yisburach that He is good to {-for} all, as it is written (Psalms 145:9) , “Hashem is good to {-for} all,” that is for all things; whether for healing, or for livelihood, or for anything. When one believes this, certainly his main endeavor ( hishtadlus ) will be after the Holy One Blessed He, and he will not chase after many schemes, for most of them do not help whatsoever, and the minute minority of them which are helpful, are not known to him and he will not be able to discover them; however, to call out to the Holy One Blessed He, this is good and beneficial for everything in the world, and this can be found always, for He blessed He is always to be found. (Likutay Moharan, Torah 14, Likutay Aitzos, Prayer 25).
