132 - There Is a Tzadik Who Is Famous Great in One Country

There is a tzadik who is renowned in one land, yet in the neighboring land is not reckoned at all. Then in a third land he again becomes renowned. Like a spring that emerges in one place, then travels underground until it re-emerges at a distant place; even while underground, it nourishes the roots of trees (Zohar Teitzei pg. 280).
