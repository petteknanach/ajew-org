Chayey Moharan

חיי מוהר"ן - חיי רבי נחמן
The Life of Our Leader Rabbi Nachman

This is the bilingual edition of "Chayey Moharan" (The Life of Rabbi Nachman), the biography of Rabbi Nachman of Breslov written by his disciple Rabbi Nathan of Breslov. The work contains stories, conversations, and events from Rabbi Nachman's life, providing deep insights into his teachings and personality.

The text is presented in both Hebrew and English side by side for comparative study. Each article corresponds to a numbered section in the original work.
