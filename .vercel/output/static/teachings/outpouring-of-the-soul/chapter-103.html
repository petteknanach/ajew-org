Outpouring of the Soul - Breslov teachings on prayer and meditation
Hisbodidus is a taste of paradise and you will see the world in a completely new way The person who merits to accustom himself to truly do hisbodidus and express his words before Hashem Yisburach properly, especially if he merits to do hisbodidus in the fields and forests, (then he) will feel with every single step he takes there, the taste of paradise ( Gan Eden ) . Also subsequently when he returns from there, (then) the whole world will be brand new in his eyes, and it will appear to him as if it is a different world, completely new, and the world will not appear at all in his eyes as it had before. (Life of Rabbi Nachman, article 107).
