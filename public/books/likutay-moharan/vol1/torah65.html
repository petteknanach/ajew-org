65 - And Boaz Said to Ruth (Vayomer Boaz El Ruth - The Field of Souls)

And Boaz said to Ruth, Have you not heard, my daughter, do not go to glean in another field, nor pass from this one, etc., let your eyes be on the field where they reap, and go after them, have I not commanded the young men not to touch you, and when you thirst, go to the vessels and drink from what the young men draw [Ruth 2:8-9] [Note: This teaching explores the spiritual field of souls, prayer, and attaining the ultimate purpose (tachlit).] 1. Know that there is a field (sadeh), and there grow trees and grasses, very beautiful and lovely, and the precious beauty of the field and its growth cannot be described, happy is the eye that has seen this. And the trees and grasses are the aspect of holy souls that grow there. And there are many, many naked souls (neshamot arumot - souls without their proper vessels) that wander and roam outside the field, waiting and hoping for their rectification (tikkun), so they may return and enter their place. And even a great soul, upon which many souls depend, sometimes when it goes out, it is difficult for it to return there. And they all seek and hope for the master of the field (baal hasadeh - the tzadik who tends to souls) who can engage in their rectification. And there is a soul whose rectification is through the death of one, or through the mitzvah and service of one. And one who wants to gird his loins to become the master of the field needs to be a measured and strong man, a valiant warrior, wise, and a great tzadik, for he must be a very great and exceedingly lofty person. And there is one who cannot complete the matter except with his death, and even for this, he needs to be very great, for there are many, many great ones who even with their death do not benefit, only if there is a very, very great and lofty person can he complete what is needed in his lifetime, for many afflictions and difficult things pass over him, but through his greatness and loftiness, he overcomes them all and performs the actions of the field as needed. And when he merits to rectify the souls and bring them in, then it is very good and beautiful to pray, for then the prayer is for their rectification. And this master of the field supervises and strives always to water the trees and grow them, and in other rectifications of the field, and to distance the trees from each other the proper distance so that one does not weaken the other, for sometimes it is necessary to show a great closeness a great distance so that one does not weaken his fellow [Correlation: The tzadik as gardener ensures spiritual growth without interference.]. 2. And know, that when the souls produce fruits (peirot - spiritual accomplishments) that fulfill the will of the Omnipresent, then the eyes of the master of the field shine, and they can watch and see in the place that is needed, "Field of Watchers" [Numbers 23:14] [Sadeh Tzofim - field of vision, linked to spiritual clarity]. But when they do not fulfill the will of Hashem blessed, G-d forbid, then his eyes are darkened, G-d forbid (Ohalot chapter 18, testimony of a minor, see there), for crying is the impairment of sight, as it is written "And the clouds return after the rain" [Ecclesiastes 12:2], and our sages, may their memory be for blessing, expounded (Shabbat 151b): This is the sight that goes after crying [Correlation: Crying impairs spiritual vision, linked to lack of divine will.]. And when his eyes shine and watch, in the aspect of the Field of Watchers above, then he can look at each and every one to bring him to the ultimate purpose (tachlit - the goal of unity and goodness), that is, he can look at the speech of each one, if it is not in its complete rectification because it is still far from the ultimate purpose, then he brings him to the ultimate purpose, and then the speech is proper in completeness, for every speech is a complete world. And when a person stands to pray and speaks the words of prayer, then he gathers buds and flowers and lovely roses, like a person walking in a field and gathering lovely roses and flowers one by one until he makes one bundle, and then gathers more one by one and makes another bundle, and joins them together, and so he goes and gathers and collects many, many beautiful and lovely bundles. Similarly, he goes in prayer from letter to letter until many letters are joined and become a speech, and so he makes complete words, and then the two words are joined, and then he goes and gathers more until he completes one blessing, and then gathers more and more, and goes from Patriarchs to Strengths, and from Strengths to Sanctities, and so further, who can extol the great splendor of the gatherings and collections that a person gathers and collects in the words of prayer. And when the speech goes out, and the speech goes out from the soul (nefesh), as it is written "And the man became a living soul" [Genesis 2:7], and its Targum: a speaking spirit [Wordplay: Root (ch-y-h - life) links soul to speech.], and the speech comes and is heard in his ears, as our sages said, may their memory be for blessing (Berachot 15a), let your ears hear what you bring out with your mouth, then the speech requests and pleads from the soul not to separate from it, and immediately when the first letter goes out, such as the letter bet from the word baruch, then it requests and pleads from the soul not to separate from it, for how can you separate from me due to the great connection and love between us, for you see my precious beauty and my radiance and my splendor and my glory, and how can you disconnect yourself from me and separate from me, it is true that you need to go further to gather more precious and great segulot (spiritual treasures or remedies), but how can you separate from me and forget me, in any case, you will see that in every place you go and come to, you will not forget me, and you will not separate from me, all the more so when he completes one word, then the entire word requests all the above and embraces and hugs it and does not let it go from it as above. And in truth, he needs and is compelled to speak many more speeches and many blessings and matters until the completion of the prayer, therefore the principle is that he needs to make one from the entire prayer, and in every speech he speaks, all the speeches of the prayer should be present there, and from the beginning of the prayer until the end, all should be one, and when he stands in the last speech of the prayer, he should still be standing in the first word of the prayer, so that through this he can pray the entire prayer, and even so, he does not separate even from the first letter of the prayer. 3. And know, that this aspect, that is, the aspect of one (echad), is the aspect of the ultimate purpose, "On that day Hashem will be one and His name one" [Zechariah 14:9], and on that day, that is, the ultimate purpose, and it is the aspect of entirely good, for one is entirely good, as our sages said, may their memory be for blessing (Pesachim 50a) on this verse "On that day Hashem will be one" etc., is He not now one? But now we bless on the bad: True Judge, and on the good: The Good and Beneficent, but in the future, they will bless on all: The Good and Beneficent, it is found that the aspect of one is the ultimate purpose, and it is entirely good, for the ultimate purpose is entirely good, for even all the troubles and afflictions and evils that pass over a person, G-d forbid, if he looks at the ultimate purpose, certainly they are not evils at all, only great goods, for certainly all the afflictions come with intention from Hashem blessed for his good, whether to remind him to return in repentance, whether to cleanse his sins, and if so, the afflictions are great goods, for the intention of Hashem blessed is certainly only for good, it is found that in all the evils and afflictions that a person has, G-d forbid, if he looks at the ultimate purpose, that is, the intention of Hashem blessed, he will have no afflictions at all, but on the contrary, he will be filled with joy, from the great abundance of good that he looks at in the purpose of these afflictions, for the ultimate purpose is entirely good, entirely one as above. And in truth, there is no evil in the world, only all is good, but the main pain that a person has from the afflictions that pass over him, G-d forbid, is only because they take from the person the knowledge until he cannot look at the ultimate purpose, that is entirely good, and then he feels the pain and suffering of the afflictions, for when he has knowledge, and looks at the ultimate purpose, he does not feel the pain of the afflictions at all as above. And in this, you will understand a hidden and concealed matter, what is imprinted in the soul of a person when he has great afflictions, may G-d spare us, such as for example when they cut off a limb of a person, G-d forbid, then he closes and shuts his eyes strongly, for this we see in reality, when a person wants to look at something far from him, then he closes his eyes and narrows and contracts the sight to aim the sight at the far thing he wants to see, and this is because the sight is a servant and messenger of the brain to bring the thing he sees into the brain, for the main sight is the knowledge, that is, to know the essence of the thing he sees, and the knowledge is in the brain. And when the brain wants to know this thing that stands before it, then it sends the sight, and the sight goes and sees the thing and brings it into the brain, and then he knows what he sees. And therefore, when they pass something before a person in great speed, he does not know the essence of the thing, even though in truth he saw the thing with his eyes literally, nevertheless, because of the speed, there was no time to bring the thing into the brain. And therefore, when the thing is far from him, the sight has no strength to go there and bring it into the brain because the other things he sees from the side confuse it, and also because of the dispersion that the sight is dispersed, through this it is weakened and has no strength to bring the thing he sees into the brain. Therefore, he needs to close his eyes, and narrow the sight and aim it at the thing needed, so that other things do not confuse it, and also so that the strength of the sight is strengthened, and it is not weak, and then he can see the far thing as above. Similarly, when one wants to look at the ultimate purpose that is entirely good, entirely one, he needs to close his eyes, and aim the looking at the ultimate purpose, for the light of this ultimate purpose is far from the person, and it is impossible to see it except by closing the eyes, that he needs to completely close his eyes, and shut them very strongly, even to press them with the finger, to close them completely, and then he can look at this ultimate purpose, that is, he needs to close his eyes from the vision of this world completely, to conceal his eyes and shut them greatly, not to look at all at the desires of this world and its vanities, and then he can see and attain the light of this ultimate purpose, that is entirely good, and then the afflictions are nullified as above, for the main afflictions are because he is far from the ultimate purpose as above. And therefore, it is imprinted in the soul of a person to close his eyes at the time of afflictions as above, to flee from the afflictions and nullify them through the looking at the ultimate purpose, that is entirely good, that this looking is impossible except through the closing of the eyes as above. And even though the person does not know anything of what he is doing, nevertheless, the soul knows everything, and therefore it is imprinted to close the eyes at the time of afflictions as above. 4. And behold, in truth, at the time of nullification (bittul) that he is nullified to the ultimate purpose that is entirely good, entirely one, then in truth the afflictions are nullified as above. But it is impossible to be constantly fixed in the aspect of nullification, for then he would go out from the human condition, and therefore it is necessary that the nullification be in the aspect of going and returning. Therefore, when the intellect returns from the nullification to the brain, which is the vessel of the intellect, then it is impossible for the brains, which are the vessels, to receive this intellect of the aspect of nullification, for it is the aspect of the infinite, which is the aspect of the ultimate purpose, that is entirely one, entirely good, and because of this, the brain feels the pain of the afflictions, for the main feeling of all the afflictions and pains, G-d forbid, is in the brain, for from the brain go out channels to all the limbs, and through this, the brain feels the pain in whichever limb it is. And know that afterward, when he returns from the nullification to the vessels, that is, the brains, then the afflictions intensify more than they were before, like two people who wrestle and fight and strengthen against each other, that when one sees that his fellow is overpowering him, then he strengthens greatly, and intensifies more, similarly, when the adversaries see that he wants to overpower the afflictions and nullify them through the nullification to the ultimate purpose as above, then they strengthen and intensify more, and therefore afterward, when he returns from the nullification, then the afflictions are greater than before, for they intensify against him because he wants to flee from them as above. But afterward, the afflictions are alleviated, and they are comforted through the renewal of the Torah (chidushei Torah) that they merit through the afflictions, for through the afflictions he comes to the aspect of nullification as above, and afterward, even though he returned from the nullification, nevertheless, from the impression that remains from the nullification, through this is made the renewal of the Torah, for through the nullification that he was nullified to the ultimate purpose and attained that all the afflictions are very great goods, through this he is filled with joy, and the joy is a vessel for the renewals of the Torah, as our sages said, may their memory be for blessing (Shabbat 88a): At the time that Israel said we will do and we will hear, sixty myriads of angels descended, and gave two crowns on the head of each one, and when they sinned etc., and the Holy One, blessed be He, is destined to return them to us, as it is said "And everlasting joy upon their head", it is found that the joy is the aspect of we will do and we will hear, which is the aspect of receiving the Torah. And through the renewal of the Torah that they merit through the impression of the nullification as above, through this they cool the afflictions afterward, for through this they extinguish the thirst of the soul (tzimaon hanefesh), for the feeling of the afflictions is the aspect of the thirst of the soul, for thirst is through saltiness, and saltiness is the aspect of afflictions, as our sages said, may their memory be for blessing (Berachot 5a), it is said covenant with salt, and it is said covenant with afflictions [Correlation: Salt and afflictions both preserve and refine, linked to the bris.], for the soul is the daughter of the intellect, for the main growth of the soul is through the intellect that grows it and rectifies it, and as it is written "Also without knowledge the soul is not good" [Proverbs 19:2]. And when the intellect is in completeness, then it produces fruits, but when the intellect is impaired, then it is the aspect of "A land of fruit into saltiness" [Psalms 107:34], and the saltiness is the aspect of the afflictions that are felt because the intellect is not in completeness as above, and it is the aspect of the thirst of the soul as above. And through the renewal of the Torah above, through this they cool the afflictions and extinguish the thirst, in the aspect of "Ho, everyone who thirsts, go to the waters" [Isaiah 55:1], "Happy is the man whom You discipline, O G-d, and teach him from Your Torah" [Psalms 94:12], for through the afflictions, he merits the renewal of the Torah as above, and this is a sign that he acted through the afflictions and received them properly, when he merits afterward to renewals of the Torah, that this is a sign that he merited the aspect of nullification to the ultimate purpose through the afflictions, and through this he merited renewals of the Torah, through the impression as above. And the master of the field above, when his eyes shine, in the aspect of "Field of Watchers", then he can look at each and every one, if he is close to the ultimate purpose, and when he sees in one that he is far from the ultimate purpose, then his prayer is not yet in completeness as proper as above, for he cannot make one from the entire prayer, and when he stands at the end of the word, he forgets the beginning of the word, and he cannot include his prayer in the aspect of one as above. And the master of the field above looks at him and brings him to the ultimate purpose, that is entirely one as above, and then he can make one from the entire prayer, and even though he stands at the end of the prayer, he is still standing at the beginning of the word of the beginning of the prayer as above. And this is the aspect of (Shabbat 104a): MNTzPK (Hebrew letters with final forms) the watchers established, hei at the beginning of a word and hei at the end of a word, watchers, this is the aspect of the master of the field, when his eyes are in the aspect of Field of Watchers as above, then he can rectify and look at those who are close to the ultimate purpose, and when they stand at the end of the prayer, they are still at the beginning of the word as above, and on those who are far from the ultimate purpose, and they are the aspect of the end of the word, for when they are at the end of the word, they are at the end of the word literally as above, and the watchers rectify them, and bring them to the ultimate purpose as above. And this is the aspect of they forgot them and returned and established them, that is, because they are far from the ultimate purpose, they forgot the beginning of the word, for they do not include their prayer in the aspect of one as above, and they returned and established them and rectified them, that it will be entirely one as above. And this is the meaning, "And Boaz said to Ruth", he is the intellect (sechel), as it is written "Wisdom strengthens the wise" [Ecclesiastes 7:19] [Wordplay: Boaz from (oz - strength) links to intellect.], Ruth is the aspect of the soul, from which come the speech of prayers and songs and praises, as our sages said, may their memory be for blessing (Berachot 7b), why is her name called Ruth, for from her came David who sated the Holy One, blessed be He, with songs and praises [Wordplay: Ruth from (r-v-h - to sate) links to spiritual speech.], "Have you not heard, my daughter", for the soul is the daughter of the intellect as above, have you not heard, that is, let your ears hear what you bring out with your mouth, that is, incline your ears and hear the request of the speech and its pleas that it requests and pleads not to separate from it as above, and this is "Do not go to glean in another field" as above, for all the letters and speeches are precious gatherings that are gleaned in supernal fields as above, and the speech requests from the soul, not to go from it to glean other gatherings as above, but this is impossible, for it needs to go and glean more as above, but "Nor pass from this one", that is, even when you go to another word, do not pass from the first word as above, and this is done through meriting the ultimate purpose as above, "Let your eyes be on the field where they reap", that is, the aspect of the looking at the ultimate purpose, for the reaping is the purpose of the plowing and sowing, "Have I not commanded the young men", this is the aspect of the closing of the eyes, that it needs to shut them and join the sight, to aim it at the ultimate purpose as above, for without this it is impossible to look at the ultimate purpose as above, and this is "Have I not commanded the young men", language of joining and connection, that it needs to command and join the sight as above, and the eyes are called young men (ne’arim), for they are the servants of the intellect as above, for the sight is a messenger and servant to the intellect as above, and this is "Not to touch you", the aspect of the plagues of the soul, for when the sight is dispersed, and sees everything before it, that is, it does not close and shut its eyes from the vision of this world as above, this is the aspect of the plagues of the soul, and it needs to command and join the young men, that is, the sight, not to look from the side at the vanities of this world, not to touch the soul, and then it can look at the ultimate purpose as above, and then through the looking at the ultimate purpose, all the afflictions are nullified as above. But afterward, when he returns from the nullification, the afflictions intensify more as above, that this is the aspect of the thirst of the soul as above, and this is "And when you thirst, go to the vessels and drink from what the young men draw", for to extinguish the thirst is through the renewal of the Torah that the brains receive, which are the vessels of the intellect, through the impression that remains from the nullification as above, and from there the soul drinks to extinguish its thirst as above, and "Drink from what the young men draw", for the young men, which are the eyes of the intellect, they draw the renewal of the Torah from the impression that remains from the looking at the ultimate purpose as above, and through this the afflictions are nullified afterward, and extinguish the thirst of the soul as above. And this is the aspect of the Garden of Eden, aspect of Moses and Aaron, garden, is the aspect of the soul, aspect of "And their soul shall be like a watered garden" [Jeremiah 31:11], Eden, is the aspect of the ultimate purpose (as our sages said, may their memory be for blessing, Berachot 34b), aspect of nullification as above, and this is the aspect of (Taanit 31a): The Holy One, blessed be He, is destined to make a dance for the righteous in the future to come, and each one points with his finger etc., dance, this is the aspect of joy, which is a vessel for receiving the Torah as above, and all this is through the nullification, that through the shining of the impression from the nullification, from there comes the Torah, through the vessels, as brought there, and this is the aspect of pointing with his finger, pointing, this is the aspect of the vision and the shining of the impression from which comes the Torah, and this is the aspect of pointing with his finger, aspect of the Torah, which is the aspect of "Finger of G-d" [Exodus 31:18].
