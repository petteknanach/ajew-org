📖 Shivchay Haran

The Praises of Rabbi Nachman

← Back to Teachings Introduction

Shivchay Haran (שבחי הר"ן - Praises of Rabbi Nachman) is the second part of the monumental work "The Life of Our Leader Rabbi Nachman." This sacred text contains testimonials, praises, and stories about Rabbi Nachman from his closest disciples and followers.

These heartfelt accounts reveal the extraordinary holiness, wisdom, and spiritual power of Rabbi Nachman, as witnessed by those who knew him intimately.
