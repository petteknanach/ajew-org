Blossoms of the Spring

נקודות טובות - Nekudos Tovos

The Holy Letters of Rabbi Yisroel Odesser (Saba)

Blossoms of the Spring (Nekudos Tovos) contains the holy letters written by Rabbi Yisroel Odesser, known as Saba Yisroel, the key bearer of the Breslov tradition who revealed the Na Nach Nachma Nachman Me'Uman petek.

These letters are called "blossoms of the stream" - flowing waters of wisdom from the holy wellsprings of Rabbi Nachman's teachings, transmitted through his faithful student Rabbi Yisroel Odesser.
