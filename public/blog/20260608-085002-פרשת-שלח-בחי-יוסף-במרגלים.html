June 8, 2026 📖 Parshat Shelach — Bamidbar 13:14

פרשת שלח — בחי' יוסף במרגלים

The Aspect of Joseph Hidden in the Spies

ב"ה

יג:יד לְמַטֵּה נַפְתָּלִי נַחְבִּי בֶּן־וָפְסִי׃

נפתלי ע"ה בגמטריא תקע"א — השנה שרבינו נפטר. נחבי נוטריקון: נחמן ישראל בער.

הראשי תיבות של: נפתלי נחבי בן ופסי, בגמטריא נ נח.

ופסי — אותיות יוֹסֵף. ואצל יששכר יש יגאל בן יוסף. יוסף היה מחבב את ארץ ישראל, ויתכן שמשה רבינו השתדל לבחור אלו אנשים שיהיו נוטים לדבר טוב על ארץ ישראל.

English Translation & Commentary

Bamidbar 13:14 — "For the tribe of Naphtali: Nachbi son of Vofsi."

נפתלי (Naphtali) with its letters equals תקע"א (571) — the year Rabbainu passed away (5571 / 1810–1811).

נחבי (Nachbi) is an acronym: נחמן חיים בער ישראל — Nachman Chaim Baer Yisroel, Rabbainu's full name from birth.

The initial letters of נפתלי נחבי בן ופסי spell נ נח ב ו — with gematria equal to נ נח (Na Nach).

וָפְסִי (Vofsi) rearranges to יוֹסֵף (Joseph). And in the tribe of Yissachar, there was Yigal son of Joseph. Joseph cherished the Land of Israel deeply, and it is plausible that Moshe Rabbeinu deliberately selected these men who would be inclined to speak favorably about the Land.

🧠 The Core Insight

The spies brought back a negative report about the Land of Israel — except for two: Yehoshua (from Ephraim, Joseph's son) and Kalev (from Yehudah). The verse hints that Joseph's aspect was already embedded in the selection of the spies themselves — the name וָפְסִי (Vofsi) contains the letters of יוֹסֵף (Joseph).

Joseph was the one who constantly spoke about the Land of Israel — he made his brothers swear to bring his bones up from Egypt. He loved the Land. Moshe Rabbeinu, in choosing the spies, sought out those who carried this aspect of Joseph — those with an inherent love for the Land.

The gematria of נפתלי = 571 — the year of Rabbainu's passing — links this entire teaching to Rabbainu Nachman, who himself made the journey to the Land of Israel and taught that the main thing is to reach the Land. The acronym נחב"י spells out Rabbainu's full name — Nachman Chaim Baer Yisroel — encoded in the verse about the one spy who would see the Land with the eyes of Joseph.

The message: even among the spies — even in the darkest moment of doubt about the Land — Joseph's light was present, hidden in the letters, waiting to be revealed through those who love the Land with all their soul.

Great blessings of Na Nach Nachma Nachman Me'Uman!
