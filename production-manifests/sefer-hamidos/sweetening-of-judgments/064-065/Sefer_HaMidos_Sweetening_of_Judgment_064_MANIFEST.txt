Sefer HaMidos – Sweetening of Judgment #64
Source authority: ajew.org
Wording notice: exact source wording preserved, including unusual phrasing and punctuation.
Hebrew exact: כְּשֶׁאָדָם מְסַפֵּר לַחֲבֵרוֹ צַעֲרוֹ, צָרִיךְ הַשּׁוֹמֵעַ לְהַחְכִּים בִּשְׁעַת הַשְּׁמִיעָה, שֶׁלֹּא יָבוֹא עָלָיו הַצַּעַר הַזֶּה.
English exact: 64. When a man tells his suffering to a friend, the listener must act wisely at the time of hearing, so that the same suffering will not come upon him.
