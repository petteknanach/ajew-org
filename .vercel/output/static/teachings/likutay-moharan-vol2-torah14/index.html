Many Fight Against Me from the Heights

Many fight against me from the heights [Psalms 56:3]. That is, that he has adversaries above, in the heights [spiritual realms]. And as our Rabbis, of blessed memory, said: Just as there are the adversaries below, so there are the adversaries from above.
