The Seven Pillars

The Seven Pillars by Reb Yitzohok Breiter - Breslov teachings
One should know that there has never been, in the history of the world, as unique a figure as the "Nachal Noveya Makor Chochmah" [Rebbe Nachman.] For he prepared the vessels/tools for our righteous Mashiach who will use them in the war to fix the world [which Rebbe Nachman says will be a war of intellect, cf cyber war.]. Amen! Ken yihee ratzon—may it be His Will!
