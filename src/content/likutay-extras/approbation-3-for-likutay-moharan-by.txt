# Approbation 3 for Likutay Moharan

## From Rabbi Avraham Yehoshua Heschel of Apta

To the holy congregations scattered throughout the exile:

I have seen the book Likutay Moharan by the holy tzaddik Rabbi Nachman of Breslov, and my heart rejoiced at seeing such a work in our generation.

The words in this book are like a well of living water, refreshing the soul and reviving the spirit. They contain both revealed and hidden Torah, practical guidance and mystical secrets.

What particularly impressed me is how the teachings address the needs of our time. In this generation of small faith and great confusion, these teachings come as a healing balm. They strengthen faith, encourage joy, and provide clear direction for serving God.

I give my full approbation to this holy work and bless all who study it with understanding hearts and willing spirits.

May God grant that through the merit of this Torah, we merit to see the face of the Messiah speedily in our days.

With blessings for Torah and fear of Heaven,
Avraham Yehoshua Heschel son of Sarah
Apta, 10 Tammuz 5568 (1808)
