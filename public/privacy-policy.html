Na Nach / aJew aNaNaCh — Privacy & Data

Developer: Pettek Nanach. Website: ajew.org.
Last updated: October 15, 2026.

This policy covers the Na Nach app, also listed as aJew aNaNaCh (Android package com.ajewananach), and explains its optional connected features. The separate per-upload Groq consent described below is introduced in version 1.7.0. Older builds may not display that separate prompt. An iOS edition is being prepared; this page does not announce App Store availability.

Reading data and local storage

Reading preferences, bookmarks, progress, downloaded texts, the optional Divine Name overlay settings and your personal Groq API key are kept in app storage on your device. This storage is not a dedicated encrypted key vault. Device backups may retain copies. The app contacts ajew.org and other content providers for online text, audio, search and website tools. These requests expose normal connection information, such as your IP address, to the service receiving them.

Optional location and reminders

Foreground location permission is optional and supports local prayer-time and compass calculations. Prayer times also offer manual city selection. Looking up a city name through the operating system may send coordinates to its geocoding service. The app does not request background location tracking. Prayer-time notifications are scheduled locally with your notification permission. Permission can be changed in device settings.

Microphone and third-party AI transcription

Whisper Speak records audio in the app cache when you grant microphone permission. In version 1.7.0, a separate prompt appears before each transcription upload. Only if you approve does the app send that recording and any reading hint to Groq, a third-party AI provider, and return the transcript. Groq receives the audio, hint, your API key and connection metadata. Microphone permission or a previously saved key does not count as approval of a new upload. Choosing Not now sends no audio in that transcription request.

The transcription service sends the recording directly to Groq, not through an ajew.org transcription server. Whisper Speak displays the returned transcript on the current screen; choosing voice search passes the words into the app search flow. Do not record or upload information you do not want Groq to process. Recordings may remain in the local app cache until cleared by the operating system or removal of app data.

Groq states that inference inputs and outputs are not retained by default. It may retain them for troubleshooting or abuse investigations for up to 30 days, or longer where legally required. Usage metadata is retained. Your Groq account's Data Controls, including zero-data-retention settings, apply. Groq describes any retained customer data as stored in the United States. These are provider policies, not an app guarantee of provider-side deletion.

Version 1.7.0 uses a personal API key entered by you rather than an embedded shared key. You can remove the saved key in Privacy & Data in the app and revoke it through your Groq account. Removing the local key does not delete previous uploads or data retained by Groq.

Embedded website tools, accounts and sharing

Embedded ajew.org pages are connected website features, not purely offline reading. They may use cookies or browser storage. Optional website registration uses Supabase authentication and processes your email address, password, display name and account/session identifiers. Website chat sends the messages you choose to submit; chat is not represented as anonymous, private or end-to-end encrypted. Website subscription features can process an account's subscription status, tier and expiry. Android retains its website subscription flow; the planned first iOS edition offers a free community view without checkout or paid activation. Hosted Stripe payment pages handle the payment details you choose to enter under Stripe's terms.

Developer-controlled website pages use Google Analytics, including within the app's website views. Analytics automatically receives page/interaction events, browser or client identifiers, device/browser information and IP-derived approximate geography. Search terms can be included when you open a full website search URL. This automatic website analytics is distinct from offline native reading, and the current website pages do not offer an in-page analytics opt-out. Native search also requests query-derived letter shards and selected document identifiers from ajew.org. Google Analytics is used to understand usage and improve the website. See Google's privacy policy and Supabase's privacy policy.

Sharing and export pass the content you select to the receiving app or service. A shared anthology link can include its selected title and source references. Browser speech recognition, where supported and explicitly started by you, may use the browser or operating-system provider's speech service; this is separate from the native Groq transcription prompt. Following a source, video, support or other external link contacts that provider, whose policies apply. App stores may provide their own download, usage and diagnostic information under your store/device settings.

Retention, account deletion and your choices

Request deletion of your Na Nach / ajew.org account and associated data, or request deletion of particular website content, by emailing naanaach@gmail.com. Send the request from the email address registered to the website account and say whether you want the whole account and associated content removed or only specified data. Do not send passwords, API keys or payment-card details. Requests are handled manually after verifying account ownership; uninstalling is not an account-deletion request.

Website account/profile information and submitted content do not have a promised automatic deletion timer; they remain until a verified deletion request or other removal. A whole-account request covers the account's login/profile data and associated submitted content under our control. Any records that must be retained for applicable legal, payment-accounting or security obligations, and the applicable retention period, will be identified when the request is handled. You may also request deletion of particular data without deleting the whole account. For provider-held data outside our control, including your personal Groq account, use that provider's controls or contact the provider. We do not claim that we can erase a user's personal Groq account or all provider logs.

Google Analytics currently has event-data retention set to 2 months and user-data retention set to 14 months, with the user-data period reset by new activity. These controls do not limit all aggregated standard reports. Groq retention is described above. Local preferences, saved reading and downloaded files remain until cleared or app data is removed; device backups may retain copies. Removing the app does not delete website accounts, submitted website content or provider-held data. You can decline optional permissions, decline each native Groq upload, remove your saved key, revoke it with Groq, and choose not to use connected website features.

Security and changes

No electronic storage or transmission method is perfectly secure. Avoid placing sensitive information in recordings, public messages or other connected tools. We will update the date on this page when this policy changes. Review the in-app disclosure before using optional features; this policy does not replace permission or upload-consent prompts.
