# Approbation 5 for Likutay Moharan

## From Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi (The Alter Rebbe)

To the lovers of Torah:

I have reviewed the book Likutay Moharan by the tzaddik Rabbi Nachman of Breslov, and I have found in it great depth and innovation in Torah.

The teachings reveal hidden connections in Torah and provide new insights into serving God. What is particularly noteworthy is how the teachings integrate the revealed and hidden aspects of Torah, showing how they are ultimately one.

The emphasis on joy, faith, and personal prayer is especially relevant for our generation. These teachings provide practical guidance for maintaining spiritual vitality in challenging times.

I give my approbation to this work and encourage its study by all who seek to deepen their connection to God.

May the merit of this Torah protect all Israel and bring us closer to the complete redemption.

With blessings for success in Torah,
Shneur Zalman son of Rivka
Liadi, 20 Tammuz 5568 (1808)
