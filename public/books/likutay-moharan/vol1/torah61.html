61 - Through Faith in the Sages (Emunat Chachamim - Faith in the Wise Ones [Sages])

Rejoiced Rabbi Shimon and said: "Hashem, I have heard Thy hearing, I was afraid" [Habakkuk 3:2]. He said: There it is fitting to fear, etc. (There in Idra, Naso, page 128) [This opening quote from the Zohar highlights the theme of proper fear and reverence, tying into faith in sages as a path that avoids misplaced fear.]

1. Through faith in the sages (emunat chachamim), one can bring out our judgment to light, for judgment (mishpat) is the central pillar (amuda de-emtza'ita - middle column in Kabbalah, representing balance) (Opening of Eliyahu [Tikunei Zohar introduction, referring to the prophetic opening by Eliyahu explaining sefirot]), that is the aspect of the middle path, which does not lean to the right or left, and this is merited through faith in the sages, which is the aspect of "You shall not deviate from the word that they tell you, right or left" [Deuteronomy 17:11] [Wordplay: "right and left" correlates with not leaning, showing faith maintains balance; root (sur - deviate) emphasizes unwavering adherence.]. And therefore, through this, the judgment comes out clear as above, in the aspect of "judgments of truth" [Psalms 19:10], that is, for all the studies that a person learns, he needs to receive and bring out from them judgments of truth, so that it not be in the aspect of crooked judgment (mishpat me'ukal - distorted ruling), that is, that he receive and learn from all the studies that he learns, judgments of conduct that he know how to behave, both for himself and for others who conduct themselves according to his opinion, each one according to his aspect, according to the governance and rabbinate that he has, whether great or small, and all this is merited through faith in the sages, which is the aspect of "You shall not deviate" etc., in the aspect of judgments of truth, that does not lean to the right or left as above. But when he damages faith in the sages, then he is judged with toil of flesh, that is with surpluses (motarot - excesses, wastes; root (ytr - extra)) (Eruvin 21b) he who mocks the words of the sages is judged with boiling excrement, as it is written "And much study is a weariness of the flesh" [Ecclesiastes 12:12] [Wordplay: "lahag harbeh" (much study) shares root with mocking, leading to "yegiat basar" (weariness of flesh), measure for measure.], and it is measure for measure, for he does not believe in their words, and mocks them, and their words are only surplus to him, therefore he is judged with surpluses, and all the judgments are from the brain (moach - mind, intellect), in the aspect of "And they feared the king, for they saw that the wisdom of G-d was in him to do judgment" [1 Kings 3:28], and the brain is according to the food, and when the body is clean, then the brain is clear, and then he can bring out judgments of truth, straight conducts, but when he is judged with surpluses, through damage to faith in the sages as above, then foul smokes rise to the brain, and mix and confuse his mind, and then he cannot bring out judgments of truth, and then judgment comes out crooked "For the wicked surrounds the righteous; therefore judgment comes out crooked" [Habakkuk 1:4] [Correlation: Wicked "surrounds" (maktir) like smokes encircling the brain, causing "crooked" (me'ukal).], that is, through the foul smokes that surround and encircle the brain and confuse it, through this judgment comes out crooked, in the aspect of "Judgments they do not know" [Psalms 147:20], that is confusion of the brains, and Doeg (worrier, from root (dag - worry)), through that his study was from the aspect of these surpluses, in the aspect of what is said about Doeg "And there was a man of the servants of Saul detained before Hashem, and his name was Doeg" etc. [1 Samuel 21:8], and Rashi explained: detaining himself etc. to engage in Torah, for his study was from the aspect of detention, aspect of surpluses, through this he did not bring out from his study judgments of truth, only crooked judgment, and therefore he judged that David is not worthy to enter the congregation (Yevamot 76b), and this was, through that his study was from surpluses as above.

2. And there are leaders who are called by the name Rabbi whose study is from these surpluses, and not only can they not even lead themselves as above, and all the more so that they cannot lead others, and they take greatness for themselves, to lead the world, one must see not to ordain them, not to give them strength and might, that they not be called by the name Rabbi, for they themselves are not so obligated, for they have a great evil inclination to lead the world, but one must guard very much not to give them strength and might, for those who give them strength and might and are ordained by them that they be called by the name Rabbi, they will have to give account and reckoning, and through ordaining a rabbi who is not worthy, through this our handwriting (ktav yad - script of hand) is weakened, and has no strength, and they give power to their handwriting, and through this they decree that there be no strength to our writings, only to their writings, and Israel will be forced to learn their writing, and through this they also decree to expel the children of Israel from the place of settlement where Israel have already settled, to places where Israel have never been, for the ordination (semicha - laying of hands) that they ordain the rabbi, and the writing, they are from one aspect, for the ordination is with hands, as it is written "And Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom, for Moses had laid his hands upon him" [Deuteronomy 34:9], and it is the aspect of the writing, aspect of writing hand, for the writing is also from the aspect of wisdom, for the letters of the writing, with them all the worlds were created "By the word of Hashem the heavens were made" etc. [Psalms 33:6], and it is written "You have made them all in wisdom" [Psalms 104:24] [Correlation: Creation through letters (word) and wisdom; each letter's form decreed by divine wisdom, showing writing as channel of cosmic wisdom.], for there is in every letter wisdom that His blessed wisdom obligated that this letter be in this form, and through it this world be created in this form, and to be conducted in that conduct that is in that world, and similarly other worlds in other form and conduct, through the form of another letter, for so is the decree of His blessed wisdom, and when the sage worthy of ordination is ordained by the name Rabbi, whose conduct is in the aspect of judgments of truth as above, then through the ordination, that then he receives his wisdom from the aspect of the hand of Hashem, in the aspect of "And Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom" etc. as above, then he brings illumination and power to our handwriting, which is also from the aspect of wisdom as above, in the aspect of "All in writing from the hand of Hashem upon me He made me understand" [1 Chronicles 28:19], that through the understanding that he receives from the hand of Hashem, which is the aspect of ordination, through this he brings illumination and power to the writing as above "And the spirit rested upon them, and they were among those that were written" [Numbers 11:26], that through being ordained then, and receiving the spirit of wisdom, through this they brought illumination to the writings, that is to our handwriting as above, and then, not only is our handwriting not subdued to their judgment, but even all their judgments of conducts, all through our handwriting, in the aspect of "To execute upon them the written judgment" [Psalms 149:9]. But when they ordain a rabbi who is not worthy, through this our handwriting is weakened, and they give strength to their writing, and all the judgments need to be through their writings specifically, which is the aspect of "Judgments they do not know", aspect of crooked judgment as above, and through this they decree to expel Israel from the place where they have settled already, for in the place where Israel dwell there from before, even in the diaspora, it is the aspect of the holiness of the Land of Israel, aspect of "A little sanctuary" [Ezekiel 11:16], for through Israel dwelling there the air is sanctified in the aspect of the air of the Land of Israel, for through our handwriting the air is sanctified in the aspect of the air of the Land of Israel makes wise (Bava Batra 158b; Zohar Pinchas 245a and in Tikkun 22), for the writing is the aspect of letters that make wise, and through the movements of the quill in the air at the time of writing, aspects of the wise letters are engraved within the air, and through it the air is sanctified in the aspect of the air of the Land of Israel makes wise, and through damaging our handwriting through the ordination that they ordain a rabbi who is not worthy as above, through this they expel Israel from the settlement, from the place where they dwelt there from before, which is the aspect of the air of the Land of Israel as above, and they expel them from there to a place of desert wilderness where there is no holiness, for Israel have never dwelt there as above.

3. And through this, the wisdom of the processes of the heavenly spheres is taken from us, to know all the changes and futures that come through the heavenly spheres, and is given to them, for initially this wisdom was given only to us, as it is written "For it is your wisdom and your understanding in the eyes of the peoples" [Deuteronomy 4:6], and our rabbis expounded, may their memory be for blessing (Shabbat 75a): What is wisdom and understanding that is in the eyes of the peoples? Say: This is calculation of seasons and constellations, for there is in this intellect, that the secret remains with us, even though they inform them of the wisdom, for certainly one must inform them of the wisdom so that they know from our wisdom that we know this wisdom, as it is written "For it is your wisdom" etc., and if so, since they inform them, it is not a secret, for also they know, but there is in this intellect, that they can inform them the wisdom and nevertheless the secret remains with us, and this is the aspect of "For it is your wisdom and your understanding in the eyes of the peoples" in the eyes of the peoples specifically, that is, that it is only in the eyes of the peoples at the time that they inform them, and immediately after that they do not know at all, only they know that the secret is with us, for we do not inform them this wisdom in its essence that it is the secret of the intercalation (sod ha'ibur - mystery of leap year calculation in Jewish calendar), only that there is in this intellect, that they can show this wisdom to their eyes only, so that they know that this wisdom is with us, but the essence of the secret of the wisdom remains with us, and this is the aspect of the secret of the intercalation that is given only to the great ones of the generation, who have great souls, for the processes of the spheres is through intellects (sichliyim - angelic intelligences governing celestial bodies), which are the angels, and each sphere has its own intellect, that is angel, that is conducted through it, and through this is the change of the walk of the spheres, that this sphere walks in a month, and in this order, and this sphere walks in a year or more, and there is a sphere that needs much time, several thousands of years until it revolves, and all according to the change of the intellects, that they change according to their distance from the cause, and similarly the spheres are conducted, and all these intellects, all receive from the general leader who is the general intellect, which is the soul that all are conducted through it "And the soul of the Almighty gives them understanding" [Job 32:8], that the soul pours the intellect into them, and therefore the soul is called heavens which is the generality of the spheres "He calls to the heavens from above", this is the soul (Sanhedrin 91b), for the soul is the general leader of all the spheres as above, and therefore the soul is called skull (galgalta - cranium, named after spheres "galgalim") on the name of the spheres of the firmament that are conducted through it, and therefore these great souls are expert in the secret of the intercalation, for who knows their conduct like the leader, and these great souls need to have a body in the aspect of "The fruit of the land" [Isaiah 4:2], aspect of the air of the Land of Israel, for when the air is sanctified in the aspect of the air of the Land of Israel, then all the fruits and produce that grow there from which the person is nourished, and from there comes the drop of seed, all is in the aspect of the Land of Israel, and from there the body is woven, in the aspect of "I was woven in the lower parts of the earth" [Psalms 139:15] aspect of the Land of Israel, and then the body is in the aspect of the fruit of the land, and then it is worthy to receive a great soul in the aspect of "He calls to the heavens from above" this is the soul "and to the earth" this is the body (there Sanhedrin), for the soul is according to the body, when the body is pure and clean, it can receive a great soul, and similarly the opposite, and therefore there are countries whose intellect is thick and gross, and there are countries whose intellect is pure and clear, and all according to the country, according to the food that comes from it, and therefore through being expelled from the aspect of the air of the Land of Israel, through the aspect above, then they cannot be woven pure bodies, and cannot receive a great soul, and through this the secret of the intercalation is taken from us, and this is what our sages said, may their memory be for blessing (Ketubot 112b) "And My hand shall be against the prophets that see vanity etc. in the secret of My people they shall not be" this is the secret of the intercalation "and in the writing of the house of Israel they shall not be written" this is ordination "and to the land of the children of Israel they shall not come" as it means, that is as above, in the writing of Israel they shall not be written this is ordination, that is through ordination that they ordain in the name Rabbi those who are not worthy, through this they weaken the writing of Israel as above, and this is "and to the land of Israel they shall not come", that is they are expelled from the aspect of the air of the Land of Israel as above, and this is "in the secret of My people they shall not be" this is the secret of the intercalation that through this the secret of the intercalation is taken from us as above, and all this through the surpluses above.

4. And through this he has no complete counsel forever, and always he cannot give counsel to his soul, and he is always in doubt, for all his counsels in the aspect of counsels of fools (Isaiah 19) counsels of women, for the filth of the surpluses rises to the heart in the aspect of "They have brought their detestable things upon their heart" [Ezekiel 14:3], and his heart becomes defiled like a toilet, which is the place of women's counsel, as the saying of Rav Ilash (Gittin 45a): Women arrange all matters in the toilet, for there is all their counsel.

5. But one who has already sunk in these surpluses, there is water that purifies from this filth, in the aspect of "And I will sprinkle clean water upon you, and you shall be clean from all your uncleannesses, and from all your idols I will cleanse you" [Ezekiel 36:25] (there 37) "And they shall not be divided into two kingdoms any more, and they shall not defile themselves any more with their idols", that through being purified from the idols and the filth above, through this "and they shall not be divided into two kingdoms", aspect of counsels, as it is written "Let my counsel be acceptable unto you" [Daniel 4:24], that is he merits complete counsel, for through the water above, complete counsels are made to him, in the aspect of "Deep waters are counsel in the heart of man" [Proverbs 20:5]. And these waters are the aspect of controversy (machloket - dispute), aspect of "Waters of strife" [Numbers 20:13], and therefore controversy is called plugta, aspect of "A river of G-d is full of water" [Psalms 65:10] [Wordplay: Plugta (Aramaic for division) sounds like peleg (river), linking controversy to purifying waters.], for from every controversy a book is made, aspect of questions and answers, for the controversy is question and difficulty, that they difficulty and ask upon him, and he returns in answer, and through this he answers and resolves the question, and from this is made aspect of book of questions and answers, for through the answer, through this several books are renewed to him, for there are several books now, and also in the future there will be more several books, and all are needed for the world, and initially when he did not have faith in the sages, then all the books were to him as nothing, for he mocked them, as it is written "Of making books there is no end, and much study" etc., that is he mocks the multiplicity of their books, and then all were as nothing to him, and when he returns in repentance on this, then a book is renewed to him each time, for all the books that initially were in his eyes as nothing are returned and counted to him, and he contemplates the controversy why they dispute so, in these words, and not in another way, and through this he contemplates how to return in repentance, and to repair the faith in the sages, for from there the controversy is drawn, through damaging faith in the sages, and according to the repentance, according to the controversy, so he returns and comes back to faith in the sages, and similarly a book is renewed to him, for he returns and it is counted in his eyes what was initially in his eyes as nothing, and so each time according to the controversy, so the repentance that he returns and comes back to faith in the sages, and similarly another book is renewed to him, for he returns and another book is counted in his eyes that initially he mocked it, and it was in his eyes as nothing as above, and therefore, through the controversy a book is made "And a book wrote the man of my controversy" [Job 31:35], that through strife and controversy a book is made. And there are tzadikim of the generation whose faith is complete certainly, and nevertheless there is controversy upon them "And he bore the sin of many" etc. [Isaiah 53:12], "And for transgressors he interceded" (there), that is he has controversy for the world, and through the controversy that is upon him, through this he repairs faith in the sages among the masses, and there are those who have controversy upon them because they have no faith in themselves, and do not believe in their Torah innovations that they innovate, and do not believe that Hashem blessed has great pleasures from their innovations, and through this that they have no faith in their own innovations, through this they are lax in their innovations, and therefore controversy comes upon them, and through this they return in repentance, and their innovations are returned and counted to them, and they return and innovate, and from this a book is made, and sometimes a book is made from this above, that through this that this asks and difficulties, and this returns in answer, and through this he answers and resolves, through this a book is made above, in the aspect of "Then they that fear Hashem spoke one with another, and Hashem hearkened and heard, and a book was written" [Malachi 3:16].

6. And through this all the contractions (tzimtzumim - restrictions, judgments in Kabbalah) are sweetened, which are the judgments, and every place that there is some judgment and contraction, is sweetened through this, through repair of faith in the sages, that through this the books of the holy Torah are multiplied, for all the sweetenings of all the contractions and judgments, is through the intellect, for all were clarified in thought (see Zohar Pekudei 254b), that the intellect is the root of the judgments, and there the judgments are sweetened, for the judgment is not sweetened except in its root (Etz Chaim Gate 3 Chapter 11), and every judgment and contraction needs to be sweetened in the intellect belonging to it, that is its root, and each contraction has an intellect through which it is sweetened, but there is supernal wisdom (chokhmah ila'ah), that all wisdoms are included there, and receive from there, and therefore there all the judgments are sweetened, for in the particular intellects, it is not possible to sweeten except in this intellect specifically belonging to this contraction, that is its root, but through the aspect of supernal wisdom that is included from all, there all the contractions are sweetened, and all the judgments all, and the Torah that comes out from supernal wisdom, for the Torah comes out from supernal wisdom (Zohar Beshalach 62a), and it can receive from supernal wisdom only when it has completeness, and the completeness of the Torah is through oral Torah, for written Torah has no completeness except through oral Torah, and therefore through the books above, that are renewed through the controversy in the aspect of "And a book wrote the man of my controversy", aspect of "Of making books there is no end", through this the Torah is completed "The tablets of stone" [Exodus 24:12], that receives illumination from the aspect of the foundation stone (even shtiyah - cosmic foundation in Temple lore) (see Zohar Vayechi 231a), for every thing in the world has another contraction in quantity and quality, and all the contractions in the world are all imprinted in the foundation stone, from which the world was founded, and all receive from it, and there is the sweetening of all the judgments, for it is the aspect of supernal wisdom, aspect of holy of holies, for the intellect is called holy (see Zohar Acharei 61a), and all receive and are included in the aspect of holy of holies, which is the aspect of supernal wisdom, that is included from all, and from there the sweetening of all the judgments, in particular and in general, for also when they sweeten the judgment in particular, in some intellect of some contraction, they need also to draw power from the supernal intellect above, so that there be power to this particular intellect to sweeten the judgment, and similarly in general, to sweeten all the judgments from whatever contraction it be, it is not possible to sweeten except through the aspect of the supernal intellect, aspect of holy of holies, aspect of foundation stone (see Zohar Vayechi 231a), and when the Torah has completeness through the books above, then it receives from supernal wisdom, in the aspect of tablets of stone, aspect of foundation stone, and then it influences all the wisdoms, and all the judgments are sweetened "He will send your help from the sanctuary" [Psalms 20:3], that the main help and salvation, which is the aspect of sweetening the judgments, is from the sanctuary, that is through the intellect that is called sanctuary as known, and this is letters shtiyah, acronyms hen tavi shaddai ya'aneni (language of the verse Job 31: "Hen tavi shaddai ya'aneni and a book wrote the man of my controversy") hen tavi shaddai, that is aspect of the impressions of all the contractions are sweetened through coming into shtiyah, aspect of foundation stone above, and this ya'aneni, aspect of the sweetening, in the aspect of "He will send your help from the sanctuary" as above, and this "hen tavi shaddai ya'aneni and a book wrote the man of my controversy" that through and a book wrote as above, that is the multiplicity of the books that are made through the controversy as above, that then the Torah has completeness, and receives from the general intellect, from supernal wisdom, which is the aspect of foundation stone as above, through this hen tavi shaddai ya'aneni, aspect of the sweetening through the aspect of tablets of stone, that receive from foundation stone as above.

7. And this is the aspect of what they travel on Rosh Hashanah to tzadikim, for Rosh Hashanah is the day of judgment of all the year, and each one comes with his holiness and his contractions to the tzadik of the generation, who is the aspect of holy of holies, aspect of foundation stone "For to Hashem are the pillars of the earth, and He sets the world upon them" [1 Samuel 2:8] (see Yoma 38b; Sanhedrin 26b), that they are the tzadikim upon whom the world is founded, and through this all the judgments are sweetened, through the aspect of foundation stone as above, and this is the aspect of the stones of Jacob, that all were included in the foundation stone (Bereishit Rabba Vayetze Parsha 68, see Zohar Noach 72b, Vayechi 231a), for the souls are aspect of stones, as it is written "The holy stones are poured out" [Lamentations 4:1], and are included in the tzadik of the generation, who is the aspect of foundation stone, and through this all the contractions are sweetened as above.

8. And through this that all the souls are included together as above, through this joy is made, in the aspect of "The light of the righteous rejoices" [Proverbs 13:9], for the soul is aspect of lamp, in the aspect of "The lamp of Hashem is the soul of man" [Proverbs 20:27], and when they are included together, light is made from them, and through this joy is made, aspect of "The light of the righteous rejoices", and this rejoiced Rabbi Shimon, that is the aspect of the joy that is made through the inclusion and the love, aspect of "The light of the righteous rejoices". He opened and said: "He who goes about as a talebearer reveals secrets" [Proverbs 11:13], that is the spirit of wisdom and he was not faithful, that is faith in the sages as above, for through that there is no faith in the sages in him, there is no spirit of wisdom, for he is judged with surpluses as above, reveals secret, this is the aspect of nullification of our handwriting that they do not receive the light of the intellect from the ordination as above, in the aspect of "With his palms he covered the light" [Job 36:32], for the letters need to receive the light of the intellect from the ordination as above, and through damage to faith in the sages, which is the aspect of he who goes about as a talebearer, through this reveals secret, that the light of the intellect is revealed and removed as above, and through this "in the secret of My people they shall not be" as above, and a faithful spirit conceals the matter, that has in him the spirit of existence, through the aspect of faith in the sages, through this conceals the matter, aspect of with palms covered the light as above.
