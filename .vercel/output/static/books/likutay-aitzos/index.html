Likutay Aitzos

Hisbodidus - Alone Time

A Collection of Remedies and Advice from Rebbe Nachman of Breslov

Likutay Aitzos presents succinct summations of the teachings of Rabbi Nachman in such a way that they can be readily and practically implemented. Rabbi Nachman himself commissioned Rabbi Nussun to compose it.

Hisbodidus (pronounced: hisboadidoos) literally means to be alone, and it takes on the meaning and function of all things holy that are best, or can only be achieved, alone.

Table of Contents

  1. Hisbodidus is a gateway to the Secrets of the Torah
  2. Personal words to G-d are Divinely inspired
  3. Yearning creates potential souls, verbalizing the yearning actualizes them
  4. Connect the heart to its point to save it from the shame of fallen desires
  5. Speaking to G-d will bring one to be embarrassed before Him with such holy connection that his face will shine
  6. Abnegation to be included in one's root
  7. At night, go alone to somewhere people never go, and completely nullify yourself to G-d
  8. Hisbodidus at night, crying to G-d and building ones good points from out of the bad, bring joy, and holds lust at bay
  9. Sighing – to regain one's standing
  10. G-d helps and provides words for one to prevail and be victorious despite all
  11. Candidly unburdening oneself to G-d evokes Divine consolation
  12. Do hisbodidus in the fields
  13. Highest virtue, Speak in your vernacular, and Set a designated hour daily
  14. Pray to attain what you learned
  15. Trying to cry is also a distraction
  16. Hisbodidus is the only way to succeed
  17. Find yourself in the Psalms and prayers
  18. Speak even without heart, always start new
  19. Speak to Hashem until your soul is about to depart
  20. Speak to Hashem like you speak to a friend
  21. Speak to G-d like an enamored toddler pesters his father
  22. Scream silently
  23. A Broken Heart Is Precious and Not Sadness
