## Batch 2: Torahs 11-20 (Part 1) — MAJOR ISSUES

### 🔴 Torah 11 — I Am Hashem He Is My Name
**Overall:** VERY POORLY TRANSLATED. Barely readable English throughout.
**Issues:**
- Seg 1: "every one from Israel needs that it be made this through him through this he can come to understandings of the Torah" — grammatically broken, meaning unclear.
- Seg 2: "straw you insert to Ephrayim? He answered them: people say, to the place of vegetables, vegetables take straw this aspect of understanding" — almost incomprehensible. The Talmudic passage about straw/vegetables is not explained.
- Seg 3: "parshas Shoftim said on repentance" — awkward phrasing. "who is the man that is fearful and soft hearted" — quote left unexplained.
- Seg 4: "his spirit dropped upon him, and they did not respond" — unclear context. "crushed crushed his measure" — doubled word, unclear meaning.
- Seg 5: "every place that you find fence of licentiousness, you find holiness" — overly literal, hard to parse.
- **VERDICT: This entire torah needs retranslation. It reads like a rough first draft or machine translation.**

### 🔴 Torah 12 — Praise to David
**Overall:** BAD. Same overly-literal style as Torah 11.
- "upper tzadik and lower tzadik because Shechinah between two tzadikim sits" — Hebrew word order
- "cannot bear spirit of Torah scholar Jewish demon because who can bear to kiss himself with carcass" — incomprehensible without context
- "jumps from this to this because from his holiness and separation jumps and leaps" — repetitive, unclear
