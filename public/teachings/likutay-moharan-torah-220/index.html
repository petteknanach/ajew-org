Likutay Moharan Torah 220

Hebrew Content

תורה מתלמידי חכמים שדין יהודאין (עיין זוהר פנחס רנ"ג:). כי תלמידי חכמים שדין יהודאין הם מקבלים תורתם מהשדין, שיש להם

English Translation

220 - THERE ARE SEVERAL PRECIOUS THINGS IN THE WORLD Know that there are several precious things in the world the wise man is very precious and so the mighty and so the rich and ruler that is who has some elevation. And know that with each one of them they can act and be saved from his trouble that if he has some trouble, G-d forbid, and he goes to one of them and tells before him until that he arouses mercy with him on him from this he can be saved.
