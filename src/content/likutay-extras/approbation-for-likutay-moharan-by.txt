# Approbation for Likutay Moharan

## From Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berdichev

To the holy community, may God protect them:

I have seen the book Likutay Moharan, containing the holy words of the great luminary, the famous tzaddik, our teacher Rabbi Nachman, may his memory be for a blessing for the life of the World to Come, grandson of the holy Baal Shem Tov.

The words in this book are words of the living God, full of fear of Heaven and love of God. They awaken the heart to serve God with truth and simplicity. Every word is like burning coals, igniting the soul with longing for the Creator.

I have found in this book great innovations in Torah, deep insights into the secrets of the Torah, and practical guidance for serving God. The author has merited to reveal hidden treasures from the storehouse of Torah wisdom.

Therefore, I give my full approbation and blessing to this holy work. May it find its way into the hands of all who seek God, and may it bring light and inspiration to all who study it.

All who involve themselves with this book will merit to draw close to God with a complete heart. May God grant that through the merit of this holy Torah, we merit the coming of the Messiah speedily in our days.

Signed with blessing for Torah and fear of Heaven,
Levi Yitzchak son of Sarah Sasha
Berdichev, 15 Sivan 5568 (1808)

## From Rabbi Zusha of Anipoli

To all Israel, my brothers:

I have reviewed the book Likutay Moharan by the holy tzaddik Rabbi Nachman of Breslov. The words in this book are like fiery flames, burning with love of God and fear of Heaven.

I have never seen such a book that combines such depth with such simplicity. The teachings penetrate to the very depths of the soul, awakening a person to true repentance and complete service of God.

The author has merited to reveal new pathways in Torah, pathways that lead directly to the King's palace. Each teaching is a ladder set on the ground whose top reaches the heavens.

I give my full support and blessing to this holy work. May all who study it merit to taste the sweetness of Torah and the joy of serving God.

May God grant that through the merit of this Torah, all Israel will return to Him with a complete heart.

Your brother who seeks your welfare and prays for your success,
Meshulam Zusha son of Miriam
Anipoli, 20 Sivan 5568 (1808)

## From Rabbi Mordechai of Neshchiz

To the holy congregations of Israel:

The book Likutay Moharan has come into my hands, containing the holy words of the great tzaddik Rabbi Nachman. I have seen in it wonders and marvels - Torah that is both deep and accessible, lofty and practical.

The teachings in this book are like a well of living water, refreshing the soul and reviving the spirit. They provide answers to the deepest questions of the heart and guidance for the most difficult spiritual challenges.

I have found particular value in the teachings about joy and simplicity. In our generation, when hearts are heavy and spirits are low, these teachings come as a healing balm.

Therefore, I add my voice to those who praise this holy work. May it be studied in all Jewish homes, and may its light illuminate the path for all who seek God.

With blessings for Torah and good deeds,
Mordechai son of Leah
Neshchiz, 25 Sivan 5568 (1808)

## Historical Note

These approbations (haskamot) were given by leading tzaddikim of the generation when Likutay Moharan was first published in 1808. Their enthusiastic endorsement helped establish the work's credibility and encouraged its dissemination.

The fact that such great figures recognized the value of Rabbi Nachman's teachings testifies to their depth and importance. Despite Rabbi Nachman's youth (he was only 36 when the first part was published), these elder tzaddikim immediately recognized the greatness of his Torah.

These approbations also reflect the unity among the tzaddikim of that generation, who supported each other's efforts to spread Torah and bring people closer to God.
