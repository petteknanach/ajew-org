# Tikkun Haklali (The General Remedy)

## Introduction

Tikkun Haklali (The General Remedy) is a set of ten psalms prescribed by Rabbi Nachman of Breslov as a spiritual remedy for impurity, particularly related to sexual transgressions. Rabbi Nachman revealed that reciting these ten psalms has the power to rectify damage to the covenant (brit) and bring about spiritual healing.

Rabbi Nachman said: "The Tikkun Haklali is a great remedy for the world. There is no sin in the world that the Tikkun Haklali cannot rectify."

## The Ten Psalms

The Tikkun Haklali consists of the following ten psalms:
1. **Psalm 16** - "מִכְתָּם לְדָוִד שָׁמְרֵנִי אֵל"
2. **Psalm 32** - "לְדָוִד מַשְׂכִּיל אַשְׁרֵי נְשׂוּי פֶּשַׁע"
3. **Psalm 41** - "לַמְנַצֵּחַ מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד"
4. **Psalm 42** - "כְּאַיָּל תַּעֲרֹג עַל אֲפִיקֵי מָיִם"
5. **Psalm 59** - "לַמְנַצֵּחַ אַל תַּשְׁחֵת לְדָוִד"
6. **Psalm 77** - "לַמְנַצֵּחַ עַל יְדוּתוּן מִזְמוֹר לְאָסָף"
7. **Psalm 90** - "תְּפִלָּה לְמֹשֶׁה אִישׁ הָאֱלֹקִים"
8. **Psalm 105** - "הוֹדוּ לַה' קִרְאוּ בִּשְׁמוֹ"
9. **Psalm 137** - "עַל נַהֲרוֹת בָּבֶל שָׁם יָשַׁבְנוּ גַּם בָּכִינוּ"
10. **Psalm 150** - "הַלְלוּיָהּ הַלְלוּ אֵל בְּקָדְשׁוֹ"

## How Rabbi Nachman Revealed the Tikkun Haklali

Rabbi Nachman revealed the Tikkun Haklali in 1805. He explained that these specific ten psalms were shown to him from heaven as a complete remedy for damage to the covenant. He emphasized that this remedy works even if a person doesn't understand the deep secrets contained within the psalms.

He said: "Even if one doesn't know the meaning of the words, the recitation itself has great power. The main thing is to recite them with sincerity and concentration."

## The Power of the Tikkun Haklali

### 1. Spiritual Rectification
The primary purpose is to rectify damage to the brit (covenant), which Rabbi Nachman taught is the foundation of a person's connection to God.

### 2. General Healing
Beyond specific rectification, the Tikkun Haklali brings general spiritual healing, cleansing from all types of impurities.

### 3. Protection
Reciting the Tikkun Haklali regularly provides protection from negative influences and spiritual harm.

### 4. Connection to David
Since King David composed the psalms, reciting them connects a person to David's soul and his quality of repentance.

## How to Recite the Tikkun Haklali

### Basic Guidelines:
1. **Recite all ten psalms** in order
2. **Say them in Hebrew** if possible
3. **Have intention** for rectification
4. **Recite with concentration** and sincerity
5. **It's preferable to say them daily** or at least regularly

### Additional Practices:
- Some recite the Tikkun Haklali after immersion in a mikveh
- It can be said as part of personal prayer (hitbodedut)
- Many recite it on Fridays before Shabbat
- Some say it at the gravesite of tzaddikim

## The Psalms and Their Significance

### Psalm 16 - Preservation
"שָׁמְרֵנִי אֵל כִּי חָסִיתִי בָךְ" - "Guard me, O God, for I have taken refuge in You."
This psalm speaks of trust in God and the joy of being close to Him.

### Psalm 32 - Forgiveness
"אַשְׁרֵי נְשׂוּי פֶּשַׁע" - "Happy is he whose transgression is forgiven."
This psalm deals directly with confession and forgiveness of sins.

### Psalm 41 - Healing
"ה' יִסְעָדֶנּוּ עַל עֶרֶשׂ דְּוָי" - "God will support him on the bed of illness."
This psalm speaks of healing and recovery.

### Psalm 42 - Longing
"כְּאַיָּל תַּעֲרֹג עַל אֲפִיקֵי מָיִם" - "As a deer longs for streams of water."
This psalm expresses deep longing for God.

### Psalm 59 - Protection
"הַצִּילֵנִי מֵאֹיְבַי אֱלֹקָי" - "Deliver me from my enemies, my God."
This psalm asks for protection from enemies and harmful forces.

### Psalm 77 - Remembering
"אֶזְכְּרָה מַעַלְלֵי יָ-הּ" - "I will remember the deeds of God."
This psalm focuses on remembering God's miracles and kindness.

### Psalm 90 - Eternal Perspective
"לִמְנוֹת יָמֵינוּ כֵּן הוֹדַע" - "Teach us to count our days."
This psalm, written by Moses, gives perspective on human life before God.

### Psalm 105 - History
"הוֹדוּ לַה' קִרְאוּ בִּשְׁמוֹ" - "Give thanks to God, call out in His name."
This psalm recounts God's kindness throughout Jewish history.

### Psalm 137 - Exile and Memory
"עַל נַהֲרוֹת בָּבֶל" - "By the rivers of Babylon."
This psalm expresses the pain of exile and commitment to Jerusalem.

### Psalm 150 - Praise
"הַלְלוּיָהּ הַלְלוּ אֵל בְּקָדְשׁוֹ" - "Hallelujah, praise God in His sanctuary."
This final psalm is pure praise, completing the rectification with joy.

## Testimonies and Experiences

Rabbi Nachman's disciples reported many instances of people experiencing spiritual transformation through reciting the Tikkun Haklali. Rabbi Natan writes in Chayey Moharan:

"Many people came to Rabbi Nachman with broken hearts over their sins, and he would tell them to recite the Tikkun Haklali. They would return with joy, having found relief and spiritual healing."

## Practical Advice for Today

1. **Start small** - If ten psalms seem like too much, start with one or two and build up
2. **Be consistent** - Regular recitation is more important than perfect recitation
3. **Combine with other practices** - The Tikkun Haklali works well with hitbodedut, Torah study, and mitzvah observance
4. **Don't get discouraged** - Even if you don't feel immediate effects, trust that the recitation is having an impact
5. **Share with others** - Introducing others to the Tikkun Haklali is a great kindness

## Conclusion

The Tikkun Haklali represents one of Rabbi Nachman's most accessible and powerful gifts to the Jewish people. It provides a practical, effective means of spiritual healing that anyone can use, regardless of their level of learning or spiritual attainment.

*"The Tikkun Haklali is a great remedy. There is no sin in the world that the Tikkun Haklali cannot rectify."* - Rabbi Nachman of Breslov
