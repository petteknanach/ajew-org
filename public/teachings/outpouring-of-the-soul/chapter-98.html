Outpouring of the Soul

Outpouring of the Soul - Breslov teachings on prayer and meditation
The Psalm recited before Sabbath is an opportunity to really articulate oneself He said: In “ Hoadoo ” (“Praise” - Psalms 107) which is said before Sabbath, before mincha (-the afternoon prayer) , it is possible to break one's heart very much, and to express all one's words there, because it speaks there regarding the hardships of the soul and regarding screaming (to Hashem) over every matter. (Words of Rabbi Nachman, article 270).
