# Approbation to Likutay Moharan

## From Rabbi Yisrael Hopstein of Kozhnitz (The Kozhnitzer Maggid)

To the holy community:

The book Likutay Moharan by the tzaddik Rabbi Nachman has reached me, and I have seen in it wonders of Torah. The teachings are like precious stones, each one reflecting divine light in a unique way.

What distinguishes this work is its ability to speak to every level of seeker. The simple person finds practical guidance, the scholar finds intellectual depth, and the mystic finds hidden secrets.

I was particularly impressed by the teachings about the power of prayer and the importance of connecting to true tzaddikim. These are essential teachings for our generation.

I give my full approbation to this holy work and pray that it finds its way into every Jewish home.

May the study of this Torah bring blessing and protection to all Israel.

With blessings for spiritual growth,
Yisrael Hopstein son of Sarah
Kozhnitz, 25 Tammuz 5568 (1808)
