# Outpouring of Soul (Likutei Tefilot)

## Introduction

Likutei Tefilot (Collected Prayers) is a work by Rabbi Natan of Breslov, the primary disciple of Rabbi Nachman of Breslov. This collection contains prayers composed by Rabbi Natan based on the teachings of Rabbi Nachman, particularly those found in Likutey Moharan.

The prayers in Likutei Tefilot are arranged according to the teachings in Likutey Moharan, with each prayer corresponding to a specific teaching. They represent a unique form of personal prayer (hitbodedut) that incorporates the deep concepts taught by Rabbi Nachman.

## The Purpose of These Prayers

Rabbi Natan composed these prayers to help people internalize and apply the teachings of Rabbi Nachman. Each prayer takes the abstract concepts from Likutey Moharan and transforms them into personal supplications to God, making the teachings accessible on an emotional and practical level.

The prayers cover a wide range of topics including:
- Faith and trust in God
- Overcoming obstacles in spiritual growth
- Dealing with depression and sadness
- Strengthening prayer itself
- Connecting to the true tzaddikim (righteous leaders)
- Personal redemption and growth

## Sample Prayer (Based on Likutey Moharan 1:1)

### Prayer for Illumination Through Torah

ה' אֱלֹקַי, רִבּוֹן כָּל הָעוֹלָמִים, אֲשֶׁר בְּרָאָנוּ לְכָבוֹדְךָ וְהִבְדַּלְתָּנוּ מִכָּל הָעַמִּים, וְנָתַתָּ לָנוּ תּוֹרָתְךָ הַקְּדוֹשָׁה.

אָנָּא ה', חָנֵּנִי וְלַמְּדֵנִי לִהְיוֹת תָּמִיד מְחֻבָּר לַשֵּׂכֶל וְהַחָכְמָה הַנִּמְצָאִים בְּכָל דָּבָר, שֶׁיָּאִיר לִי הַשֵּׂכֶל שֶׁבְּכָל דָּבָר, וְאֶזְכֶּה לְהִתְקָרֵב אֵלֶיךָ עַל יְדֵי כָּל דָּבָר.

תֵּן בְּלִבִּי לְהָבִין שֶׁהַתּוֹרָה הִיא הַיַּעֲלַת חֵן, הַמַּעֲלָה חֵן וַחֲשִׁיבוּת לְיִשְׂרָאֵל לִפְנֵי כָּל הַצְּרִיכִים, בֵּין בְּרוּחָנִיּוּת בֵּין בְּגַשְׁמִיּוּת.

זַכֵּנִי לִלְמֹד תּוֹרָתְךָ בְּכֹחַ וּבִגְבוּרָה, וּלְחַזֵּק עַל יְדֵי זֶה אֶת הַמַּלְכוּת הַקְּדוֹשָׁה, בְּחִינַת נוּן, שֶׁתְּקַבֵּל מֵהַחָכְמָה, בְּחִינַת חֵית. וְיִתְחַבְּרוּ הַחֵית וְהַנּוּן וְיִהְיֶה נַעֲשֶׂה חֵן, וְיִהְיֶה אוֹר הַלְּבָנָה כְּאוֹר הַחַמָּה.

אָז אֶזְכֶּה שֶׁדְּבָרַי יִהְיוּ דִּבְרֵי חֵן, וְיִכָּנְסוּ דְּבָרַי וְתַחֲנוּנַי לְלֵב מִי שֶׁאֲנִי מְבַקֵּשׁ מִמֶּנּוּ, וְיִתְקַבְּלוּ תְּפִלּוֹתַי וּבַקָּשׁוֹתַי לְפָנֶיךָ.

הָאִירָה פָּנֶיךָ עַל תּוֹרָתְךָ הַקְּדוֹשָׁה שֶׁאֲנִי לוֹמֵד, וְתֵן בִּי כֹּחַ לְהַכְנִיעַ אֶת הַיֵּצֶר הָרָע עַל יְדֵי הַתּוֹרָה, שֶׁהִיא כֻּלָּהּ שֵׁמוֹתֶיךָ הַקְּדוֹשִׁים. וְתַצִּילֵנִי מֵרוּחַ שְׁטוּת וְשִׁגָּעוֹן, וְאֶזְכֶּה לִהְיוֹת מֵאַשְׁרֵי תְמִימֵי דָרֶךְ, הַהוֹלְכִים בְּתוֹרַת ה'.

## Structure of Likutei Tefilot

The prayers are organized in two parts:

### Part One
Contains prayers corresponding to the first part of Likutey Moharan (Torahs 1-60). These prayers focus on fundamental concepts of faith, prayer, joy, and personal service of God.

### Part Two  
Contains prayers corresponding to the second part of Likutey Moharan (Torahs 61-282). These prayers address more specific spiritual challenges and deeper concepts.

## The Power of Personal Prayer

Rabbi Nachman emphasized the importance of hitbodedut (personal, spontaneous prayer in one's own words). Likutei Tefilot serves as a bridge between structured prayer and personal expression, providing a framework for people to learn how to speak to God from the heart while incorporating the deep wisdom of Breslov teachings.

## How to Use These Prayers

1. **Study the corresponding teaching** from Likutey Moharan first
2. **Read the prayer** slowly and thoughtfully
3. **Personalize the words** to your own situation
4. **Speak to God** as if talking to a close friend
5. **Repeat regularly** to internalize the concepts

These prayers are not meant to replace the traditional prayer service but to supplement it with personal, heartfelt communication with the Divine.

*"The main thing is prayer. All the remedies and advice that Rebbe Nachman gave are all included in prayer."* - Rabbi Natan of Breslov
