Likutay Moharan Torah 189

Hebrew Content

תורה הזאת; דברי התורה - הם בחינת דבורי ה

English Translation

189 - ONE MUST BEWARE VERY MUCH FROM SADNESS AND LAZINESS One must be extremely vigilant against sadness and laziness, for the serpent's primary bite manifests as sadness and laziness, in the aspect of (Isaiah 65:25): "And dust shall be the serpent's food" – dust representing sadness and laziness, derived from the element of dust as explained in the books.
