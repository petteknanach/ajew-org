Na Nach account & data deletion

For the Na Nach / aJew aNaNaCh app (Android package com.ajewananach) and its ajew.org website accounts. Developer: Pettek Nanach.

You can request deletion of your website account and associated data, or request deletion of particular data without deleting the whole account. No app installation or website sign-in is needed to send a request.

Request account or data deletion by email

If the button does not open an email app, email naanaach@gmail.com with the subject “Na Nach account and data deletion request”.

What to include

  1. Send the request from the email address registered to the ajew.org account, so account ownership can be verified.
  2. Say whether you want the entire website account and associated data deleted, or only particular data. For a content-only request, identify the relevant message or content and its page/link if available.
  3. Do not send passwords, Groq API keys, recordings or payment-card details. If you cannot access the registered email address, explain that without sending sensitive credentials.

What the request covers

A whole-account request covers the account's login/profile information, including its registered email and display name, and associated submitted website content under our control. A request can instead identify particular submitted content while retaining the account. Requests are handled manually after ownership verification; this page is a request route, not an automatic account-delete API.

Retention and exceptions

Website accounts and submitted content do not have a promised automatic deletion timer; they remain until a verified deletion request or other removal. Any account, payment-accounting or security records that must be retained for applicable legal obligations, and the applicable retention period, will be identified when the request is handled. Google Analytics has separate event/user retention controls and aggregated reports, explained in the privacy policy.

Uninstalling the app does not delete a website account. Local preferences, downloads and cached files can be removed through the device's app-data controls; device backups may retain copies. A server-side request does not itself clear every local device or backup.

Personal Groq and other provider accounts

The app uses your own Groq API key for optional transcription. Removing that key locally or requesting deletion of an ajew.org account does not erase prior Groq uploads or delete your personal Groq account. Use Groq Data Controls or contact Groq for provider-held information. Other external providers handle their accounts and legally retained records under their own policies; our request route covers information under our control.

You can also find the request email in the app under Privacy & Data → Website features & deletion. Offline reading does not require a website account.
