Tales of Rabbi Nachman

← Back to Teachings Shivchay Haran

Stories (סיפורים) by Rabbi Nachman of Breslov are profound parables that contain deep spiritual wisdom and teachings about the soul's journey back to God.

These allegorical tales speak of kings, princesses, lost souls, and transformative journeys — each carrying layers of meaning for those seeking devekus (closeness to God) and repentance.
