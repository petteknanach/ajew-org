Welcome

Rebbe Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810) was a great-grandson of the Baal Shem Tov, founder of Chassidus. His teachings continue to inspire and guide millions of people around the world in their service of Hashem.

This site is dedicated to spreading the light of Rebbe Nachman's teachings — his Torah, stories, and practical guidance for living a life of joy, faith, and connection.
