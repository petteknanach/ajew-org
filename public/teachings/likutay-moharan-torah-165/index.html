Likutay Moharan Torah 165

Hebrew Content

תורה הזאת ספר איזה זמן מקדם מעשה נוראה שראה במקום שראה, בהקיץ או בחלום, ואמר שה

English Translation

165. “Love your neighbor as yourself—I am G-d” [Leviticus 19]. Receive lovingly all evils and sufferings befalling you, knowing per your deeds, despite all sufferings and evils, He still mercifully deals with you—for more was due, G-d forbid. Thus, “love your neighbor”: love the evil (evils, sufferings) befalling you, G-d forbid—accept lovingly, as yourself (per your deeds). “I am G-d,” Mercies' Master, still merciful as above.
