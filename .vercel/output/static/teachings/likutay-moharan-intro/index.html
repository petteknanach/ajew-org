Introduction to Likutay Moharan

← All Teachings ← Likutay Moharan Index

Likutay Moharan (ליקוטי מוהר"ן - Collected Teachings of Rabbi Nachman) is the magnum opus of Breslov Chassidus, containing the Torah teachings given by Rabbi Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810) throughout his life.

These teachings span a vast range of topics: faith, joy, prayer, repentance, Torah study, hitbodedut (seclusion with God), simcha (joy), and the path to true closeness with the Creator. Rabbi Nachman's unique approach combines deep Kabbalistic wisdom with practical advice for daily life.

The Likutay Moharan is divided into two volumes:

Volume 1 contains Torah teachings given from 1805 until Rabbi Nachman's passing in 1810. These torahs were written down by his close disciple, Rabbi Natan of Breslov, and include both public teachings and more esoteric insights.

Volume 2 contains earlier teachings, including the famous Torah 48 "The Whole World is a Very Narrow Bridge," and many other profound discourses on faith, joy, and Moshiach.

Each torah in this collection is presented with its complete text - no truncation, preserved exactly as translated from the original.
