With the help of Heaven [ב״ה].
Uzi Meshulam, of blessed memory—some say that he was one of the thirty-six righteous ones—fought against the Zionists. He wrote this letter in their prison—on the day that Saba Yisrael passed away! And he began with the expression found in the holy note: “My precious disciple”:
With the help of Heaven [בס״ד], the eighteenth of Marḥeshvan, 5755. “I will thank You, for You answered me, and You became my salvation” [Psalms 118:21].
Its sign is: “My strength [‘uzzi] and the song of Y-H” [Psalms 118:14]. May this be a Year of the Vengeance of the Blessed Name [תהא שנת נקמת השי״ת] upon the ‘erev rav, from that evil seed of Amalek—may their name and memory be blotted out!
Words of Torah from the Torah of our master Rabbi Naḥman son of Feiga, the memory of the righteous and holy one be for a blessing—“the flowing stream, the source of wisdom” [naḥal nove‘a mekor ḥokhmah]—may his merit protect us, amen.
The Blessed Name had compassion upon us and brought down into this world the soul of our master, of blessed memory, which issues from the first three [sefirot] of ‘Atiq, for his soul is from that Reisha de-la Ityada‘ [“Head that is Not Known”], concerning which no man has any standing, in the mystery of David the King, the Messiah. Into his hand was entrusted the rectification of the impurity of the blemish of the Covenant, in the mystery of “Before the sun, his name shall be Yinnon” [Psalms 72:17], which is the mystery of “Yod,” “nun”: the mo’aḥ [mind/brain] of the Gate of Nun [the Fiftieth Gate], from which derives the principal freedom to depart from all the constraints [meitzarim], as it is written: “And fifty [va-ḥamishim] the Children of Israel went up from the land of Egypt” (Exodus 13)—that is, precisely through the revelation of the Fiftieth Gate, the mystery of the illumination of Binah, Israel departed from the nakedness of Egypt, which is the impurity of the blemish of the Covenant, as is known. And it is known that, in the mystery of Messiah, Messiah is exceedingly zealous for the Blessed Name and wages war to eradicate the blemish of fornication from the world, as stated: “The breath of our nostrils, the Messiah of the Lord” [Lamentations 4:20]—opposed to, and in order to break and eradicate, the letters of “fornication” [ni’uf; ניאוף, an anagram of אפינו, “our nostrils”].
And therefore, all the blemished souls from the generation of the Flood—which is the blemish of the Covenant—and the generation of the Dispersion—which is heresies—returned and were reincarnated in these generations. And although many thousands upon thousands, and the greater part of many myriads, of the blemished souls have already been elevated and rectified by the unique ones of the generations who existed until now, awesome true righteous ones, nevertheless there remained such holy souls as had fallen into exceedingly, exceedingly immense blemishes, whom even those righteous ones, the unique ones of the generations, did not have it in their power to rectify, for reasons incomprehensible and unattainable to our human intellect. Until He had compassion and brought down into this world the soul of our master, Rabbi Naḥman, the mystery of the soul of David the King, the Messiah; and he is hidden in Y-H, in Messiah, in the mystery of “If there is no flour [kemaḥ], there is no Torah” [Avot 3:17]!
And as we said above, his soul is from that Reisha de-la Ityada‘ [“Head that is Not Known”], concerning which no man has any standing, and into his hand was entrusted the rectification of this sin, and the gathering and gleaning of all those banished and scattered throughout the four corners of the earth, and their restoration to their root. He introduces into them the point of light of the New MaH [מ״ה החדש], the illumination of the Forehead, the World of Rectification, with such exceedingly, exceedingly wondrous and awesome renewal and revelation, until they are transformed into an entirely different person, as is written (Isaiah 12): “And you shall say on that day: I will thank You, O Lord, for You were angry with me; Your anger shall turn back, and You shall comfort me [ותנ׳ח׳מ׳ני]. Behold, God is my salvation; I shall trust and not fear, for my strength [‘uzzi] and the song of Y-H, the Lord, and He became my salvation.
“And you shall draw water with gladness from the wellsprings of salvation. And you shall say on that day: Thank the Lord, call upon His name; make His deeds known among the peoples; make mention that His name is exalted. Sing to the Lord, for He has performed majesty; this is made known throughout all the earth. Exult and sing, O inhabitant of Zion, for great in your midst is the Holy One of Isra-El” [Isaiah 12:3–6]. And in the Targum there, if, with the help of the Blessed Name, we scrutinize each and every word, we shall be able to understand wonders of the mysteries and secrets of Torah. For after one merits becoming acquainted with this true righteous one, who is among the choicest of the righteous, and receiving from him awesome Torah—new teaching flowing from the inward unification—matters that have not yet been revealed in the world until now,
all the descents, falls, and castings-down that befall one who draws near to the true righteous one were worthwhile.
And this is what is intimated in the Targum: “And I shall give thanks before You, O Lord, for because I sinned before You, Your wrath is upon me; and when I return to the Torah of the Concealed Ancient One, Your wrath will turn away from me, and You will have mercy upon me. In You I trust, and I shall not tremble because of any mortal, for the Holy Great One said to cause His blessed Shekhinah to dwell within me and throughout the earth, and to bring redemption near for the entire holy people and for all the worlds.”
And it is brought in the holy books that when the soul of the true righteous one, such as the aforementioned righteous one, must descend into the world, the Accuser and his hosts accuse and contend that if it should come down into this lowly world, there will no longer be anything for the Accuser and his hosts to do. And the Accuser comes with claims before the Creator of the World: “For what purpose did You create us?” and so forth, if You bring this soul, which issues from the first three [sefirot], from that Reisha de-la Ityada‘ [“Head that is Not Known”], concerning which no man has any standing. And they answer the Accuser that this soul must certainly descend into the world!
Your end, O Accuser, has arrived, according to the verse and in the wording of the verse in Ezekiel 7: “An end has come; the end has come” . . . “Now the end is upon you, O Accuser”; and you, the Accuser, have one choice: to seek proposals and stratagems for yourself with which to contend against this holy soul, since it will be of no avail—this soul will descend into this world.
The Accuser went forth, then came and said that there is already a counsel and stratagem, and this soul can descend.
And our holy master says that the counsel and stratagem of the Accuser became known throughout all the worlds: namely, the magnitude of the spread of concealment and controversy concerning this holy soul.
“And they will speak about him such falsehoods and lies as could not be believed if related,” and every kind of hindrance and accusation will be related concerning him and will spread over him, and he will be in great hiddenness and concealment from the world—this is the mystery of the soul of our righteous Messiah, who will be revealed speedily in our days.
Master of the Universe, I, Your servant Uzi Azulai, son of Rabbi David and Tziona, wait with consuming longing for the revelation of the illumination of our righteous Messiah. Please help me. You are my Father, my God, and the Rock of my salvation: [help me] to rectify my ways thoroughly, so that, Heaven forbid, I shall not be among those who delay the coming of the Redeemer; and may we merit, speedily in our days, amen, that our righteous Messiah come at our head, and that we ascend to Jerusalem with everlasting gladness, to raise His Shekhinah from the dust.
And may it be His will that all our severe sufferings be accepted favorably before You, to raise the Shekhinah from the dust. “You have dealt well with Your servant, O Lord, according to Your word” [Psalms 119:65]—act according to Your word, and not according to my request. Amen; so may it be His will.
Yet I shall raise a prayer to You—a prayer to You, my Father. You are my God and the Rock of my salvation. Instruct me what I should ask; teach me what I should request, for the sake of sanctifying Your name in the world.
“O glorious, comely One, radiance of the world, my soul is sick with love for You. Please, O God, please heal her by showing her the pleasantness of Your radiance; then she will grow strong and be healed, and she shall be Your everlasting handmaiden” [from Yedid Nefesh]—“W I T H  E V E R L A S T I N G  G L A D N E S S!” Yes, Father, surely You know!
And if so, our master Rabbi Naḥman, the memory of the righteous and holy one be for a blessing, may his merit protect us, amen, says that the mystery of the soul of our righteous Messiah will be in great hiddenness and concealment from the world, somewhat as our Sages, of blessed memory, said (Sanhedrin 98): “The Son of David comes only through diversion of attention,” for he will already be in the world, but will be in concealment.
And every matter that merely touches this soul will come with very great heaviness, exceedingly so, and with immeasurable, immense hindrances, from within and from without; and it will be clothed in several kinds of garments, and so forth. And there will be no possibility for people and for the House of Israel to draw near to this soul except specifically through self-sacrifice,
as is written in Likkutei Moharan, Part I, section 152: For the entire power of the Samekh-Mem—the Accuser—and his hosts, who act to delay and prevent these souls from drawing near to this true righteous one, the soul of Messiah, operates by reason of the souls of those very people: because those people blemished the blemish of the Covenant, through the emission of seed in vain [zera‘ le-vatalah], may the Merciful One save us.
Therefore, through their sin they gave power to the Samekh-Mem and his hosts; and therefore they themselves caused all the hindrances and delays, for they are still clothed in filthy garments, from which the hindrances come, as do the darkness and crookedness in the heart, with doubts, confusions, and immense difficulties, as the Accuser makes sport of them—for example:
After all, this “great one” said such and such, may the Merciful One save us, about the righteous one; and a second “great one and kabbalist” slandered the true righteous one with such and such, may the Merciful One save us; and so forth.
Until many rise against each person who wishes to enter into holiness and to draw near in truth to the true righteous one.
And it is impossible to break all the hindrances that spread over everyone who wishes to enter and to receive and suckle from this hidden light (the true righteous one), except specifically through self-sacrifice, as our master Rabbi Naḥman, of blessed memory—may his merit protect us, amen—expounds through his disciple in Hilkhot Hekhsher Kelim [Laws of Rendering Vessels Fit], law 4. For it is impossible to receive from this lofty soul in the degree of service of the Blessed Name—for that soul is the “Wondrous Counselor,” whose counsels are wondrous, awesome, and exceedingly deep, from the aspect of the thousand lights—except through humiliations and much shedding of blood: one must be exceedingly, exceedingly humiliated before one merits receiving from the light of the holy Torah of the true righteous one, the breath of our nostrils.
For it is impossible to suckle from, and be sanctified by, the true righteous one except through literal self-sacrifice, as we see perceptibly the magnitude of the hindrances, sufferings, and coverings that must pass over everyone who wishes to draw near to the truth; he must endure sufferings to the point of the crushing of the soul.
And concerning this it is said in Psalms 44: “For Your sake we are killed all the day; we are accounted as sheep for slaughter” [v. 23]. And likewise it is said in the holy Zohar, pericope Terumah: “The Torah endures only in one who puts himself to death over it.” That is: “one who breaks all the hindrances, endures all the sufferings, and effects literal self-sacrifice in order to draw near (not to become acquainted!) to the true righteous one, to his Torah, and to his people; then he is secure from stumbling” (and it likewise expounds in Likkutei Halakhot, [Hilkhot] Basar be-Ḥalav [Meat and Milk], law 5). And thus it calls to those who draw near to the true righteous one, each according to his filthy garments that require rectification. On the one hand, his soul and his good inclination bring him merit to become acquainted with this true righteous one, the Hidden Light, who can rectify him with such a wondrous and awesome rectification that he will merit the revelation of the light of the New MaH (the explanation of “to-ki” [תוּכי], as in a parrot [tukki; תוכי], which voices that which was caused to be heard by it and understands nothing: the intended meaning of “parrot” is Uzi A. Meshulam)—our holy Rabbis said “the New MaH” in מד״ע [an unresolved acronym], and among them, and foremost, our master the Ari, of blessed memory, may his merit protect us, amen, in Etz Ḥayyim, in the Gate of Nesirah [Severance], [said] that Malkhut came into being from the lower segment of Hod of Arikh Anpin.
And this is the “fourth spirit” mentioned in Idra Rabba, folio 132, and from there [comes] the King Messiah. And from him they will apprehend and understand wisdom, for then the light of the moon will be elevated. Thus far the words of the Idra Rabba. It is also known that the Messiah will merit, speedily in our days, amen, the “illumination of the Forehead,” which is called “Will,” continually!
And it is known that the “illumination of the Forehead” is the mystery of the Name of the New MaH, which is called “Hadar,” and it is intimated (Genesis 36:39): “And Hadar reigned in his stead.” And this is what King David intimated in Psalms: “Splendor [hod] and majesty [hadar] You set upon him” [Psalms 21:6]. “Hod”—this is “the fourth spirit,” which is the mystery of Hod of Arikh Anpin; and “Hadar”—this is the mystery of the “New MaH”; “You set upon him”—upon the King Messiah.
If so—as we said above in the name of our holy master—on the one hand, the soul and good inclination of one who draws near to the true righteous one bring him merit to become acquainted with this “Hidden Light,” the true righteous one, who can rectify him, with a wondrous and awesome rectification, for the service of the Blessed Name according to his root: such a wondrous and awesome rectification that he will merit the revelation of the light of the New MaH, which is the revelation of the Hidden Light from the six days of Creation, and so forth, as above. Yet, on the other hand, by reason of the garments of his “filthy clothing,” the evil inclination of the one drawing near, and his blemishes in the blemish of the Covenant, may the Merciful One save us—on the other hand, from the side of the “Other Side,” as he draws near he hears all kinds of opposition concerning the true righteous one; all kinds of hindrances, difficulties, and doubts spread over him, and major controversies concerning this true righteous one reach his ears. If, then, there is intellect in his mind, he must know, believe, and understand—our master says—that all these are a rebuke to him for having blemished the drops of his brain.
And concerning this sin it is said (in Proverbs): “None who come to her return, nor do they attain the paths of life” [Proverbs 2:19]. For if he had not blemished and sinned in the blemish of the Covenant, and if his brain had not been blemished, quarrels and controversies concerning “the true righteous one” would not have been made audible to him; and all the quarrels and controversies, imaginings and enticements, came to him only from the side of the “Other Side,” in order, Heaven forbid, to distance him from the “Hidden Light”—the “true righteous one.”
And therefore, when he merits truly to understand, and not, Heaven forbid, to deceive himself, then even if he hears opposition, controversies, and enticements to distance himself from this “Hidden Light”—the true righteous one—and even if, in his own mind, thoughts should several times arise that perhaps, Heaven forbid, it would be better to flee from this true righteous one, then he will immediately settle his mind, consider, and have very great compassion upon himself:
Where is he in the world? For they seek to distance him from the source of his vitality, from the supernal pleasantness, from “the flowing stream, the source of primordial wisdom,” the mystery of the soul of our righteous Messiah: NNMḤ [ננמ״ח] = KEMAḤ [קמח; “flour”], “If there is no flour [kemaḥ], there is no Torah” [Avot 3:17]. He is the river that purifies from all stains in the world. Through this itself he will break the crookedness in his heart, and will pay no regard to any hinderer or delayer, even if the hindrances are from himself. And thus, with the passage of time, he will merit entering within the gates of holiness, and will see many wonders and all good forever.
He who awaits his disciples—with the blessing, “May the Lord give strength to His people” [Psalms 29:11], that they arrive very soon—Uzi A. son of Rabbi David Meshulam.

Na Naḥ Naḥma Naḥman me-Uman.
